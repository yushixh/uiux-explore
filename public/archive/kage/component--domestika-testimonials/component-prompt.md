## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, community, and brand are, then apply these principles to that context.

Build a teacher or contributor showcase section with a simple heading and a horizontal carousel of profile cards. Use a very light neutral background, generous vertical whitespace, and three visible cards inside a constrained container. Each card should have a small portrait on the left, name and role beside it, and compact metrics below. Use rounded light-gray cards, subtle shadows, dark text, and circular previous/next controls at the outer edges. Add pagination dots beneath the cards with a strong active dot. Support swipe and keyboard interaction, and let cards clip gracefully at the viewport edges on smaller screens.

Never: copy the reference's names, occupations, profile photos, exact metrics, logos, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
