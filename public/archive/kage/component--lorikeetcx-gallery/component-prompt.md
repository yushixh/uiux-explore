## Before you start
Ask what visual best represents the user's product and brand, then apply this treatment to an original asset.

Build a single-image visual section following the hero. Center one landscape product or lifestyle image in a wide whitespace-rich band. Keep the image smaller than the viewport, with softly rounded corners and a subtle border or shadow so it feels intentional rather than like a full-bleed banner.

Design rules:
- Layout: centered image, approximately 440px wide at the reference scale, with a 16:9-ish aspect ratio; preserve generous whitespace around it.
- Image treatment: object-fit cover, radius 16–20px, 1px translucent border #D9D9D9, very soft shadow.
- Background: white or warm white #FFFFFF.
- Responsive behaviour: image width 70–80vw with a sensible max-width; never crop important faces or product details.
- Interaction: optional gentle hover lift, but no distracting animation.

Never:
- Never reuse the reference photograph, product imagery, logo, copy, or illustration.
- Never fill the entire section with the image.
- Never add captions unless the user's content requires them.
