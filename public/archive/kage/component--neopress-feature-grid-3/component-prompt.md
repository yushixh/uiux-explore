## Before you start
Ask what technical capabilities the user's product genuinely provides and which outcomes matter to its audience. Then create an original technical feature section that makes complex infrastructure understandable.

### Design language
- Begin with a strong left-aligned editorial heading and two or three short explanatory paragraphs, then present three alternating rows pairing product evidence with a concise benefit statement.
- Use diagrams, dashboards, or code-like surfaces as evidence panels, but keep them simplified and legible; each row should have one clear takeaway.
- Use a neutral technical palette: warm white (`#fbfaf7`), pale sand (`#f1eee6`), white panels, near-black (`#191918`), muted gray (`#706e68`), and a clay/coral accent (`#d97759`).
- Combine serif display headings with compact sans-serif labels. Use hairline borders, 2–6px radii, and consistent media aspect ratios.
- Stack rows on mobile, preserve reading order, and provide captions/alt text for diagrams and screenshots.

### Never
- Never reuse logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never claim performance or search results without evidence, or fill the section with unexplained jargon.
