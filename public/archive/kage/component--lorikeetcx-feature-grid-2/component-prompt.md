## Before you start
Ask what channels and operational systems the user's product connects to, then design an original introduction around those priorities.

Create a spacious feature-introduction section. Place three small channel indicators or icons above a centered serif headline, followed by a concise explanatory sentence. The visual goal is to make breadth of channel support feel clear before the deeper workflow explanation appears.

Design rules:
- Layout: centered narrow content column with icons evenly spaced above the heading.
- Typography: serif headline 32–42px with a compact two-line maximum; sans-serif body 13–15px.
- Icons: simple line or product-specific icons in muted grey/lavender, each with a small label.
- Colour: white background, black text #111111, muted grey #7B7B7B, optional pale lavender #EEEAFE.
- Spacing: 28–40px between icon row and heading; 12–18px between heading and body; generous vertical padding.
- Interaction: channel indicators may be selectable tabs if multiple stories exist.

Never:
- Never reuse the reference icons, wording, brand mark, or product name.
- Never overwhelm the section with a grid of tiny features.
- Never use unsupported channel claims.
