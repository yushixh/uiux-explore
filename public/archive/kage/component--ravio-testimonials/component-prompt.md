## Before you start
Ask who the user's customers are, what measurable outcome is credible, and whether the content should be a carousel or a static story. Apply these principles to the user's product and brand.

Build a customer proof section with a left-aligned two-line editorial serif heading and a dark or outlined case-study CTA aligned to the right. Under it, provide a lightweight customer selector row with subdued wordmarks or text labels and previous/next controls. The active story should be a three-part card: a pale warm metric panel, a wider quote panel, and an image/avatar panel. Use #f5f6f0 for the card, 1px #e6e8e2 dividers, 10px corner radius, serif for the metric and main heading, sans-serif for labels, and generous internal padding. Include clear attribution and make carousel controls accessible with visible focus states. On mobile, stack the three panels and turn the selector into a scrollable row.

Never: copy the reference's customer logos, names, testimonials, people, portraits, claims, copy, or imagery.
