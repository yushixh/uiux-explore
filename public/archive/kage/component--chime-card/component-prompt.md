## Before you start
Ask what adjacent product capability the user's audience should discover and what brand colors and visual assets represent it. Then build a promotional split card.

Build a wide, softly tinted promotional card with a two-column layout and substantial whitespace. Place a compact eyebrow, a three-line bold headline, short explanatory paragraph, and filled CTA on the left; place a product screenshot, data visualization, or abstract visual on the right. Use an off-white or very light gray-green surface around #F5F7F3, 16px radius, and minimal shadow. Use dark green typography #064A35, 30–38px headline sizing, 15–16px body copy, and a bright green CTA around #19C477 with 6–8px radius. Keep the text column around 42% width and the visual around 38%, vertically centered. Make the visual decorative but understandable and stack it below the copy on narrow screens.

Never: copy the reference's investment wording, product UI, chart, logos, product name, imagery, or exact copy.
