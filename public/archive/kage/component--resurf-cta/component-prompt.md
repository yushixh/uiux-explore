## Before you start
Ask what the user's primary conversion is, which platforms or plans are available, and what final reassurance removes hesitation. Apply those answers to this closing CTA.

Create a centered closing call-to-action with a two-line editorial serif heading, one dark rounded button, and a small muted line beneath it describing eligibility, pricing, trial, or platform support. Keep the section visually quiet and spacious so the action feels intentional after the product showcase. Use `#fafafa` background, `#202020` heading and button, white button text, and `#858585` supporting text. Use a 36–46px heading, 14px button text, 10px radius, 16px horizontal padding, and 72–104px vertical spacing. Include a clear hover state and a visible keyboard focus ring; on mobile, let the button reach a comfortable tap width.

### Never
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never add multiple competing CTAs at the very end.
- Never hide important pricing or platform conditions in tiny text.
