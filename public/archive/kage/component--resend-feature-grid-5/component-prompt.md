## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand voice and visual identity are. Apply the following principles to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Create a technical trust section on near-black. Use a large two-line heading and supporting statement at the top, followed by a 3-column grid of six to nine capability items. Each item should have a simple line icon, concise title, two- to three-line explanation, and optional link. Keep alignment strict, with 32–48px row gaps and consistent icon/title spacing. Use white headings, #969696 body text, #202124 icon strokes, and no filled cards unless needed for grouping. Add a subtle horizontal glow or divider near the section boundary. Stack into one column on mobile and preserve readable line lengths.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
