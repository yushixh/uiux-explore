## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to their identity rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a dark customer-logo strip around 100–120px tall. Place a thin top and bottom divider inside a centered max-width container and distribute 6–8 customer marks evenly across one row. Render marks in desaturated gray with low contrast so they support rather than dominate the page; use responsive wrapping or horizontal scrolling on small screens. Use #050606 background, #242827 rules, and #8a8f8d marks with generous horizontal spacing.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
