## Before you start
Ask what the user's business audience, product, and conversion goal are, then create an original version of this structure.

Build a centered business-focused section on white. Start with a bold 32–40px headline, one or two lines of supporting text, and a quiet text or soft-pill CTA. Below it, add a wide rounded media frame with a warm orange-to-yellow gradient around #FF641E to #FFE53D, a centered product interaction mockup, and a small circular play control near the lower-right corner. Use a max content width near 690px, 24px corner radius, 64–96px vertical spacing, and black typography. Keep the section calm and editorial above the vivid media, and make the media a responsive video or image slot rather than hardcoded artwork.

### Never
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never copy the social-feed mockup, people, or exact gradient artwork.
