## Before you start
Ask which forms of credibility are real and appropriate for the user's product—customers, investors, certifications, or communities. Then create an original trust strip using only approved assets.

### Design language
- Center a short eyebrow such as a credibility label above a single horizontal row of marks within a softly tinted panel.
- Keep the strip visually quiet: small uppercase sans-serif label, low-contrast monochrome marks, generous horizontal spacing, and strong vertical padding.
- Use warm white (`#f8f7f3`) against the page's off-white (`#fbfaf7`), muted gray logos (`#9b9a94`), and a faint border (`#eeeae2`).
- Preserve supplied logo aspect ratios, use accessible alt text, and let the row wrap or become a horizontal scroll region on mobile.
- Avoid heavy shadows and keep the component short enough to act as a transition rather than a second hero.

### Never
- Never reuse logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never invent endorsements or imply affiliation that the user cannot substantiate.
