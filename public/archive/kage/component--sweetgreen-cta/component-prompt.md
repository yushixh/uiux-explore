## Before you start
Ask what business or team-oriented offering the user's product has and what actions visitors should take. Use the user's brand voice, colour palette, and imagery.

Create a wide split-layout promotional section on a soft sage background with approximately 56% text column and 44% image column. Add a small eyebrow, an oversized multi-line headline, and three vertically stacked service links; each link should have a bold underlined label followed by one or two lines of supporting copy. Align all text to a shared left edge and give the section generous 28–40px padding. Place one tall, rounded-corner lifestyle image on the right, using object-fit cover and a crop that feels editorial. Keep the panel airy, with the image vertically centered and a clear responsive stack on mobile. Links should gain a colour or underline transition on hover.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
