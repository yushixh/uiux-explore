## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that context rather than reproducing a restaurant-booking site.

Create a horizontally scrollable discovery-card section for items available now. Use these rules:
- Place a section heading on the left and a quiet secondary link on the right inside a max-width container.
- Use five equal cards on desktop, each with a 16:9-ish image, 1px #e1e3e6 border, 6px radius, and restrained shadow or elevation.
- Establish a hierarchy: bold item name, muted category/location line, rating row with a warm red accent #c83c51, activity line, and an outlined availability button.
- Keep card padding around 10–12px; image corners should follow the card radius. Use charcoal #292d35 and muted #69717a.
- Add a bookmark icon in the image/content boundary and a circular next-arrow control overlapping the right edge. Make carousel controls keyboard accessible and support touch scrolling.
- Preserve consistent card heights with line clamping for long descriptions and keep the action aligned near the bottom.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, imagery, or exact visual assets from the reference.
