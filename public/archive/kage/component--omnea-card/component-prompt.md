## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand personality and primary conversion goal are. Then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a case-study feature band on a white or warm-white background. Begin with a small category label, an editorial headline, two compact outcome metrics, and a right-aligned outlined story CTA. Place a wide rounded video/image thumbnail beneath with a clear play button, then add a two-column caption row: context on the left and a short attributed quote on the right. Use 6px corner radius, a thin #e4e4df border, muted gray secondary text, and a documentary visual treatment rather than glossy marketing art. Keep the video accessible with a real button, visible focus state, and transcript or caption affordance.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
