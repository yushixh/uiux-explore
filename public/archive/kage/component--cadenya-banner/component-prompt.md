## Before you start
Ask for the user's core promise or memorable product mantra. Apply the composition and emphasis rules below using entirely original wording.

Build a typographic statement banner on a dark green canvas. Use one oversized serif sentence split across two lines, with a short phrase highlighted in a pale lime rectangular outline or tinted block. Keep the text left aligned within a wide content gutter, allow the line breaks to feel intentional, and leave substantial breathing room above and below. Use off-white text (#f4f2e5), lime emphasis (#d8ef87), dark green background (#0d5d46), and a hairline border or faint grid. The display type should be expressive, around 42–58px on desktop with tight line-height; the emphasized phrase should retain the same family but gain color and a thin frame. Scale fluidly and avoid awkward overflow on small screens.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
