## Before you start
Ask what action the user's product most wants visitors to take and what trust message supports it. Apply this structure with original brand content.

Build a wide rounded CTA panel inside a dark page section. Use a subtle charcoal gradient (#141516 to #101112), a 1px border (#2c2e30), 14–16px radius, and a faint technical grid or glow in the background. Arrange the panel as a two-column split: on the left, a small uppercase eyebrow, a large 3-line headline, supporting paragraph, and two buttons; on the right, a subdued product/interface preview that supports the message without overpowering it.

Use bright white headings, gray copy, one light primary button and one dark secondary button. Keep the panel padded 48px on desktop and stack it on mobile, moving the visual below the copy. Add hover and focus states while preserving strong contrast.

Never: use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, imagery, or exact CTA labels.
