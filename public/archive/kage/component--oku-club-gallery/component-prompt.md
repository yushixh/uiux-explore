## Before you start
Ask what recent user activity or content the product can showcase and whether the experience should be a static strip or interactive carousel. Match the user's brand and content model.

Build an edge-to-edge horizontal activity gallery beneath the social-proof section. Show a row of compact cards with a thumbnail or cover area, a small avatar/status marker, muted metadata, and one or two lines of activity text. Allow the row to clip or horizontally scroll so it feels like a live stream rather than a rigid grid. Keep the card height consistent and the visual density low.

Use white cards, `#ededed` borders, 8px radius, subtle shadows, near-black text, gray metadata, and 12–16px gaps. Provide arrow controls or touch scrolling where appropriate, with visible focus states and a reduced-motion fallback.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
