## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to that context rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a second content rail for newly available items. Reuse the same listing-card system—rounded image, small label badge, favorite affordance, title, rating, and muted metadata—but give the section its own heading and enough separation from the previous rail. Keep card dimensions consistent, use white space rather than heavy dividers, and support horizontal scrolling with a visible next control. Use a restrained palette: black text, gray metadata, white cards, and one warm accent for ratings.

Never: use the reference's logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or branding.
