## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a closing conversion section on the dark background. Center a strong two-line headline, a primary filled CTA, and a secondary contact-style action. Beneath it, place two equal audience cards with distinct titles, one-sentence descriptions, and compact buttons so visitors can choose the path relevant to them. Use #171721 overall, #20202b cards, #6d63ff primary action, white headings, #9c9ca9 body text, 8–10px radius, and generous separation between headline and cards. Make the hierarchy obvious and ensure each card has a clear focus state.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
