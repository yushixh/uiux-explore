## Before you start
Ask what evidence supports the user's product, whether it is open source or independently verifiable, and what proof readers need before converting. Use only substantiated proof.

Design a credibility section with a two-column intro: a strong headline on the left and a short proof-oriented paragraph plus dark pill CTA on the right. Under it, place four equal white cards with a compact promise, a small metadata label, and a short explanation. Use `#f5efe3` background, white cards `#fffdf8`, `#171714` text, muted `#6c665c`, and a black CTA `#111210`; use 16–20px radii, 20px gaps, subtle borders, and a calm editorial rhythm. Ensure the CTA has a clear external-link or repository affordance when appropriate, but do not imply endorsements that do not exist.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
