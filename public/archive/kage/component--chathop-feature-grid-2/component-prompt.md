## Before you start
Ask what trust, privacy, safety, or transparency commitments are true for the user's product. Turn only those verified commitments into cards.

Create a compact trust section on a pale lime background. Add a small uppercase eyebrow, a centered heading, then a four-column grid of equal cards. Each card gets a short bold principle title and two or three lines of explanatory text, followed by a single underlined link to a fuller policy or trust page.

Design language: background #efffd5, near-black #10140c text, muted gray-green body copy, white cards around #ffffff, 1px border #e7eee0, and 10–14px radius. Use a 28–36px heading, 13–14px card titles, and 12–14px body text. Cards should have consistent padding around 18–22px, a very soft shadow, and equal heights. Collapse to two columns then one column on mobile. Underlined links should show a visible hover color shift.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
