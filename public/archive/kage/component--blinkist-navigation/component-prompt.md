## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a responsive top navigation for a modern learning or subscription product. Use a clean white bar about 52–64px tall, a compact brand mark area on the left, one lightweight category/menu control beside it, and utility links plus one high-contrast primary CTA on the right. Keep the content in a centered max-width container with generous horizontal padding. Use a dark navy text colour around #062f3b, a vivid mint-green CTA around #20d982, subtle bottom border #edf1f0, and 8px button radius. Typography should be friendly geometric sans-serif: medium-weight navigation labels and semibold CTA text. On small screens collapse secondary links into a menu while preserving the primary action.

Never use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
