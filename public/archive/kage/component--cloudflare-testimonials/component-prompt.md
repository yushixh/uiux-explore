## Before you start
Ask which customer evidence the user's product can substantiate and whether logos, quotes, metrics, or case studies are appropriate. Apply the structure to that evidence.

Create a social-proof section on white with a centered trust headline around 32–40px, a short muted subtitle, and a restrained horizontal logo cloud beneath it. Use neutral monochrome or low-saturation logos with consistent optical sizing and generous gaps. Follow with a featured testimonial card: large quotation styling, a short specific quote, attribution with role/company, and a small avatar or brand mark area. Add optional carousel controls and a second row of customer names or logos only when there is enough evidence. Use thin borders (`#ededed`), subtle shadow, 12–16px card radius, and responsive horizontal scrolling for logo rows.

### Never
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never fabricate customers, quotes, or business outcomes.
- Never overcrowd the logo cloud or make every logo equally dominant.
