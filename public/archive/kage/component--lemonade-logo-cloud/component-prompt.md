## Before you start
Ask what verified credentials, partnerships, or institutional facts the user's product can legitimately present, and apply this structure to those facts.

Build a three-column trust-credential band. Each column should include an original badge, certification mark, or simple geometric emblem, a concise heading, and two or three lines of explanatory copy. Use a white background, centered alignment, charcoal #454545 typography, muted gray #777777 descriptions, and restrained monochrome marks with one optional brand-accent detail. Set a max width around 800px, 48–72px column gaps, 24–32px heading type, and 14–15px body text. On mobile stack the columns with generous separators or spacing. Link credentials to verification pages where possible and do not present unverified claims.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
