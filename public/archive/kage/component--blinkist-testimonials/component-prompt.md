## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a testimonial section with a centered two-line headline and three horizontally arranged member stories. Each story should pair a rounded portrait tile with a small audience badge, a star rating, and a short quote with selective bold emphasis. Use an editorial layout rather than generic cards: vary the portrait positions slightly, add oversized low-contrast quotation marks as decoration, and maintain a generous 24–36px gap. Keep the background white, use navy #073544, soft lavender accents #eeeaff, muted body text #4f5859, and 16px rounded image corners. Add a centered CTA underneath and stack stories cleanly on mobile.

Never use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
