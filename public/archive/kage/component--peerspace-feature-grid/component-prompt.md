## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then adapt this discovery pattern to their taxonomy.

Create a spacious white two-column discovery section. Put a large left-aligned heading and supporting sentence above a multi-column list of selectable activity categories, followed by a dark filled browse button. Place one tall, high-quality image in the right column with a tiny vertical editorial caption along its outer edge. Use black text, muted gray secondary labels, 48–64px desktop heading typography, 24px column gaps, and a restrained 4–8px button radius. On mobile, stack the image below the category list and make categories wrap or become horizontally scrollable.

Never: use the reference logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or distinctive brand assets.
