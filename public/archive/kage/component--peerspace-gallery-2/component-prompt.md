## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then create an article module for its content strategy.

Build a white editorial article carousel. Start with a small uppercase eyebrow, a bold section title, and a dark view-all button; place previous/next arrows on the opposite side. Below, show several horizontally arranged cards with consistent thumbnail ratios, small category labels, article titles, and thin bottom rules. Keep the cards compact and image-forward, use 24–32px gaps, black text, muted gray metadata, and no heavy shadows. Support horizontal swipe, keyboard controls, and responsive card widths.

Never: use the reference logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or distinctive brand assets.
