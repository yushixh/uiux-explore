## Before you start
Ask what audience the signup serves, what incentive or message should appear, and which digital platforms the user's product supports. Match the user's brand system.

Create a compact utility section on an off-white background with two equal columns separated by a fine vertical divider. The left column contains a small uppercase eyebrow, one sentence of value-oriented signup copy, and a wide outlined email field with placeholder text and a right-aligned arrow submit control. The right column contains a matching eyebrow and vertically stacked platform links with arrows, optionally accompanied by a small product/app mark supplied by the user. Use 28px side padding, restrained dark text, thin olive-grey borders, and a comfortable 16–20px internal rhythm. Stack columns and remove the divider on mobile. Include visible focus states and accessible labels.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
