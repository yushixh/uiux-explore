## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a compact token-introduction section with a numbered section heading and a three-column grid of color cards. Each card should have a large color swatch, token name, hex/value metadata, and one short usage note. Use a strict baseline grid, thin gray borders, a paper-colored background, monochrome labels, and one or two brand accents chosen for the user's product. Keep cards flat, square or 2px radius, with 14–20px padding and 10–12px labels. Make the swatches prominent and ensure the grid collapses cleanly on small screens.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations or imagery from the reference.
