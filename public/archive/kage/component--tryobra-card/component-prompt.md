## Before you start
Ask what the user's product demo should show, how long it takes to understand, and what low-friction action should launch it.

Build a centered demo card on a near-black page. Use a short centered heading and supporting sentence above a wide bordered preview frame. Inside the frame, combine a simple product title treatment, a prominent orange play button, and a couple of small utility controls or metadata labels. Use a 1px warm-white border, subtle dark-on-dark layering, square corners, and 32–48px internal padding. Make the play control keyboard accessible and provide hover/focus feedback without relying on imagery.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
