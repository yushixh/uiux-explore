## Before you start
Ask whether the user's audience is migrating, upgrading, or switching from an existing tool, and what reassurance is truthful. Then write an original single-action CTA.

### Design language
- Use a centered, spacious composition with a soft warm background and a two-line editorial heading that moves from a muted premise to a dark, decisive statement.
- Add a short reassurance line beneath the heading and one dark filled button; keep the action singular and obvious.
- Use cream (`#f8f6ef`), near-black (`#171716`), muted gray (`#77746c`), and a restrained coral accent for optional emphasis. Use a 2–4px button radius and generous vertical padding.
- Keep the CTA width narrow enough to feel intentional, and scale the heading fluidly for small screens.
- Include clear hover, focus, and loading states; do not force a modal or multi-step form for a simple migration action.

### Never
- Never reuse logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never use fear-based migration claims, multiple competing buttons, or unsupported guarantees.
