## Before you start
Ask what conversion action the user's product should drive, what promise is most compelling, and what reassurance removes friction. Then write the CTA for that product.

Create a spacious centered CTA band with a slightly lighter charcoal surface than the rest of the page. Use a large two-line serif headline, a small muted reassurance sentence beneath it, and a light pill-shaped primary button. Keep the content vertically centered with generous top and bottom padding and no competing elements. Use #1a1a1a for the band, #f1f0eb for the headline, #a4a49f for supporting text, and #f2f2ee for the button with dark text. The button should have a subtle hover lift and a clear focus ring. Maintain a restrained, premium editorial feel rather than adding decorative graphics.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
