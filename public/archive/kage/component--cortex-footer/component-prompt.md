## Before you start
Ask what brand identity, navigation groups, legal links, and community channels the user's product needs. Use those answers to create an original footer.

Build a dark footer with a thin top border and generous top padding. Use a four-column desktop layout: a brand block with a small mark, product name, and one-line description, followed by three groups of small navigation links. Use uppercase 9–10px group labels, 11–12px muted links, and generous vertical spacing. Add a bottom rule and a compact copyright/license row at the bottom.

Keep the background #090a0a, borders #202223, primary text #e8e8e5, and secondary text #77797b. Links should brighten on hover and show visible keyboard focus. Collapse into a two-column or stacked layout on mobile while keeping the brand block prominent.

Never: use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, imagery, or exact link labels.
