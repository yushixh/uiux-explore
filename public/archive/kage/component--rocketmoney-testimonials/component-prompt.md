## Before you start
Ask what evidence the user's product has from customers and what review policy, tone, and brand colors should be respected. Apply this structure with original customer content.

Design a testimonial section in a broad pale-neutral rounded panel. Use a left column with a 36–48px trust headline, a short supporting statement, a five-star row, and a small disclosure note. On the right, arrange several white quote cards in a staggered two-column mosaic; each card includes a short quote, customer name, and optional star rating. Use #F5F5F5 for the panel, #FFFFFF cards, #111111 text, #5F5F5F metadata, and a warm brand accent for stars. Use 16px card padding, 12–16px card radius, subtle shadows, and 72–112px panel padding. Keep quotes readable, support overflow-safe masonry or CSS grid behavior, and stack the trust summary above cards on mobile.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
