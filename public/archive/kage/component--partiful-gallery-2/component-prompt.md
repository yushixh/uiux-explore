## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a community image collage:
- Use a full-width band with rows of candid photos arranged edge-to-edge in varied landscape and portrait crops.
- Allow small overlaps and slight rotations for an organic scrapbook feel, while preserving a clean overall horizontal rhythm.
- Keep gutters minimal, use consistent object-cover treatment, and add a subtle color grade so disparate images feel cohesive.
- Provide meaningful alt text or mark decorative images appropriately; do not rely on the collage alone to communicate content.
- On mobile, use a shorter strip or horizontally scrollable gallery with touch-friendly behavior.

Never:
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never use people’s photos without permission.
