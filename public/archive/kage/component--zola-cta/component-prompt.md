## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a two-column support CTA with a rounded lifestyle or team image on the left and a simple text block on the right. Use an off-white background, a thin vertical divider, a short editorial heading, reassuring supporting copy, and one high-contrast dark pill button. Align the copy vertically toward the center of the image and keep the composition spacious rather than adding extra controls. Use #FAFAF7, #111111, and a configurable accent; use a 20–24px image radius and a 999px button radius. Collapse to a stacked layout on small screens, placing the text after the image.

Never:
- copy logos, product names, or reference copy
- reuse reference imagery or illustrations
- reproduce the exact brand styling
