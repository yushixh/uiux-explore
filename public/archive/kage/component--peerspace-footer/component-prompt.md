## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then structure the footer around its real navigation and legal needs.

Create a black multi-column footer with a large stacked brand lockup at left and several concise link columns for company, discovery, providers, regions, support, and apps. Use soft-white headings and muted gray links, generous column spacing, and a thin horizontal divider above the legal row. Finish with language/privacy links on the left, copyright on the right, and a row of small social icons. Use #050505, #FFFFFF, and #8A8A8A, 13–15px link typography, 32–48px section padding, and responsive collapse into accordions on mobile.

Never: use the reference logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or distinctive brand assets.
