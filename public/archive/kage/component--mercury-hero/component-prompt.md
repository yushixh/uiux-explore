## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a full-bleed hero directly beneath the navigation. Layer a dark translucent gradient over an atmospheric, product-relevant visual so the text remains legible. Center the composition, use a strong 38–56px headline with tight line-height, a short muted supporting paragraph, and a primary filled blue-violet CTA beside a low-emphasis text link. Add ample top and bottom breathing room and ensure the visual continues behind the content. Use #171721 and charcoal overlays, white type, muted lavender-gray secondary text, and 8px controls. On mobile, stack the actions and keep the headline to two or three lines.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
