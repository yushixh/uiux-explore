## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, which customers or partners may be shown, and what trust signal matters most. Apply the following structure with the user's own assets and brand.

Build a compact social-proof section on a dark page. Start with a small muted editorial sentence and an inline underlined link, then place a wide rounded media panel below. Overlay a centered serif heading near the top of the panel and arrange two balanced rows of monochrome customer or partner marks with generous spacing. Add a dark gradient over the image so marks remain legible. Use charcoal backgrounds (#090909), warm white heading text (#f0eee8), muted copy (#92928e), low-contrast panel borders (#282828), and an atmospheric image treatment that is darkened and desaturated. Keep logos optically balanced rather than mechanically equal in size.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
