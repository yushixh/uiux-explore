## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand personality and primary conversion goal are. Then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a featured content section with a left-aligned heading and a small outlined “view all” control on the right. Below, show three equal article cards with wide 16:9 thumbnails, concise titles, and a tiny author or metadata line. Use varied but invented editorial imagery, 6px image corners, a clean white canvas, and restrained typography: 22–28px heading, 15–16px article title, 11–12px metadata. Add hover elevation or image zoom only subtly, and make the complete card keyboard accessible.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
