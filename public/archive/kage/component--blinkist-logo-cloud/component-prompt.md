## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a full-width trust strip with a very pale blue background around #dcecff. Center a small bold statement above a horizontally distributed row of monochrome partner wordmarks or brand placeholders. Keep marks optically balanced rather than forcing equal widths, use muted navy/blue-gray #557286, and provide 40–64px vertical padding. On smaller screens allow horizontal scrolling or wrap the marks. Use accessible text alternatives and do not let the trust strip compete with the primary CTA.

Never use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
