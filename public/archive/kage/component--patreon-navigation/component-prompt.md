## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Build a transparent desktop navigation over a full-bleed visual. Use a three-part alignment system: compact secondary links on the left, a centered brand mark, and utility actions on the right. Keep the bar about 64–72px tall with generous horizontal padding, small readable sans-serif text, a subtle translucent treatment, and pill-shaped outlined and filled actions. Preserve strong contrast against changing imagery, make the search and menu actions keyboard accessible, and collapse gracefully on mobile.

Never: use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
