## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand personality and primary conversion goal are. Then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a customer proof section beginning with a small label and an oversized statement about a meaningful business outcome. Below, use a split panel: a documentary-style customer image or abstract product-context visual on the left and a concise quote with attribution on the right. Underneath, present three equal stat cells with a small customer/category label, a very large metric, and a one-line qualifier. Use #fbfbfa with pale #e6e6e1 dividers, black/charcoal typography, and one muted sand image panel. The quote should be readable at 14–16px; metrics should be 34–48px with tight tracking. Ensure the image, quote, and stats form one coherent proof narrative and stack cleanly on mobile.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
