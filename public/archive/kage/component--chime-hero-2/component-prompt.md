## Before you start
Ask what mission the user's product has, what community it serves, and what original photography or artwork is available. Then create a distinctive mission section.

Build a full-width dark-green brand statement section. Place a wide, centered community image or original visual in the upper portion, blending it into the background with careful cropping and optional gradient treatment. Below it, add a small uppercase eyebrow in a vivid mint accent and a centered 36–48px bold mission statement in white, constrained to about 720px. Use a deep background around #003B2B, generous vertical padding, and a calm, editorial rhythm. Keep the image accessible with meaningful alt text and ensure the statement remains readable at all breakpoints. Avoid unnecessary controls; this is an emotional brand section rather than a conversion form.

Never: copy the reference's people, photography, logo, mission statement, product name, imagery, or exact wording.
