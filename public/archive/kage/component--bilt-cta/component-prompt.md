## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are. Adapt this closing conversion moment to their product's primary action.

Create a full-width rounded CTA panel with a subtle original texture or gradient behind it. Center a two-line headline, a short supporting statement, and one prominent button. Keep the panel distinct from the page without becoming a loud color block; it should feel like a confident invitation after the proof section.

Design language: panel background should use a blue-black gradient such as #132331 to #0b1822 with a very subtle line texture; heading #f3f6f7, body #b5c0c7, and CTA #a9d8f4 with dark text. Use 16px radius, 1px rgba(255,255,255,.08) border, 56–88px vertical padding, 40–52px heading size, and 16px body size. Keep the content max-width around 520px and ensure the button has a clear hover, focus, and disabled state.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
