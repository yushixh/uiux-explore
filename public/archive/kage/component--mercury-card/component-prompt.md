## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a media-mention section with a simple heading and three equal dark cards. Each card should show a publication placeholder, a concise editorial headline, and a small circular arrow or link control. Use #242430 cards on #171721, low-contrast borders, 10px radius, 16–20px padding, and clear hover/focus states. Keep the cards aligned to a shared baseline and let headlines occupy a consistent height. On mobile, turn them into a horizontally scrollable row with snap points and visible partial next-card affordance.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
