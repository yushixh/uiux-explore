## Before you start
Ask what measurable customer outcome, data source, and brand style the user's product has, then apply this layout using original content.

Build a proof-of-savings section with a centered headline, short explanation, a cluster or responsive grid of circular statistics, and one primary CTA. Use a white background, charcoal text #454545, and pale neutral circles such as #f5f5f5 with one or two circles using a very light tint of the brand accent. Each statistic should have a large percentage or value, a short label, and a source or qualifier where needed. Arrange circles with varied but controlled sizes on desktop, then normalize them into a two-column grid on mobile for readability. Keep the section vertically spacious, use 32–38px heading type and 14–16px labels, and place a compact rounded #f50087 CTA below the data. Do not imply guaranteed savings; include transparent methodology or footnotes.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
