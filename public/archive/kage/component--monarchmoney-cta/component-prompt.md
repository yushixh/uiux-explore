## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Build a horizontal split CTA card with text on the left and a product-in-use image on the right. Include a small uppercase eyebrow, a large serif offer heading, concise explanatory copy, and a rounded orange action button. Use a white left surface, a carefully cropped image, 16px outer radius, and subtle border/shadow. Keep the CTA visually distinct but not louder than the final orange banner below. On mobile, stack the image below the offer content and preserve a comfortable tap target.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
