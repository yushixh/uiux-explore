## Before you start
Ask what behind-the-scenes story, educational content, or editorial feature the user's product should promote. Ask which social or content destination matters to the brand.

Build an editorial feature with a two-column layout on a muted sand background. On the left, use a compact eyebrow, a large 3–4 line headline, a short descriptive paragraph, and a low-positioned text link with a simple social icon and arrow. On the right, use a tall portrait-oriented original image with a straight or subtly rounded edge, cropped to fill the column. Keep the heading oversized but lightweight, use a compact sans-serif for body text, and create a strong vertical rhythm with a large gap between the intro copy and the follow link. At mobile widths, stack the image beneath the text and keep the follow link immediately after the content.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
