## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Design a focused product feature band with a heading, short paragraph, and three compact supporting points on one side, paired with one tall portrait-oriented visual on the other. Use a high-contrast abstract visualization or product mockup with a restrained gradient, not a busy illustration. Align the text vertically to the visual center. Keep the dark canvas (#171721), use white headings, #9d9daa body text, #343543 dividers, and a 10px visual radius. Add subtle hover motion to the visual and maintain readable text at narrow widths by stacking columns.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
