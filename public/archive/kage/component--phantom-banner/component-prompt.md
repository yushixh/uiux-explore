## Before you start
Ask what legal, financial, privacy, or compliance disclosures the user's product genuinely requires. Do not invent claims; use the component only when appropriate.

Create a low-emphasis disclosure band with centered text in a constrained max-width column. Use a pale page background, dark gray-violet text at approximately 14–16px, relaxed line height, and italic styling only where legally or editorially appropriate. Provide generous top and bottom spacing so the notice does not feel cramped, and ensure it remains readable on mobile with sensible line wrapping. Do not use decorative imagery or a competing CTA.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
