## Before you start
Ask which product principles matter most to the user's audience, especially around trust, privacy, accessibility, or business model. Use the user's own terminology and legal guidance.

Create a text-led principles section with a narrow centered content column. Organize it as repeated two-column rows: a short bold label on the left and a concise explanatory paragraph on the right. Use generous vertical spacing and a thin divider only when needed. Keep the language scannable and let the section communicate how the product behaves, not just what it does.

Use a white background, near-black `#111111`, gray body text `#5f5f5f`, 14–16px body size, 24–30px row gaps, and a restrained sans-serif interface typeface. On mobile, stack each label above its explanation.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
