## Before you start
Ask what the user's final conversion goal and brand voice are, then write an original closing CTA around them.

Build a compact centered closing CTA with one memorable serif headline and one dark pill-shaped button. Keep the message direct and confident, using whitespace to make the action feel important.

Design rules:
- Layout: centered single-column block with headline and CTA vertically aligned.
- Typography: serif headline 34–48px, tight line-height; optional supporting sentence at 14–16px.
- Colour: white or warm white background #FFFFFF; near-black text #111111; CTA #171717 with white label.
- Spacing: 20–28px from headline to button, 70–110px vertical section padding.
- Interaction: button has clear hover/focus states and opens the appropriate product flow.

Never:
- Never reuse the reference headline, product name, logo, copy, imagery, or illustration.
- Never add multiple competing actions.
- Never use urgency claims unless they are truthful.
