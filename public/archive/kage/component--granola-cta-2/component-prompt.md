## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than copying the reference.

Create a final conversion panel centered within a quiet light section. Use a large rounded warm-white card with a very subtle shadow or border, generous internal padding, a prominent serif headline, one short explanatory paragraph, and two actions: a filled dark primary button and an outlined secondary button. Keep the panel narrower than the viewport so the surrounding whitespace frames it. Use near-black text (#222222), warm off-white (#f8f8f3), dark charcoal button (#292929), and a thin neutral outline (#bcbcb4). Buttons should be keyboard accessible, clearly labeled for the user’s product, and stack or wrap gracefully on mobile.

Never:
- use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference
- copy the reference offer, pricing language, or exact card styling
- include more than one dominant primary action
