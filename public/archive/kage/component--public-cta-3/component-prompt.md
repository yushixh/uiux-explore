## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Create a final account-opening CTA on a black canvas. Use a two-part composition: left-aligned serif headline and short dark-mode signup button on the left, a wide product/device scene or generic interface image on the right, and a small star-rating quote beneath or beside the headline. Keep the image partially cropped so it feels cinematic, but ensure it never overwhelms the conversion action. Use #050505, #F4F4EF, #A4A4A4, subtle #202020 borders, a 999px button, and 32–64px internal padding. Add responsive stacking with the CTA first on mobile.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
