## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a press/social-proof strip:
- Use a clean white background with generous top and bottom padding.
- Arrange 3–5 publication or customer marks in an evenly spaced horizontal row, each paired with a short quote or descriptor.
- Render marks in monochrome or subdued gray so the section feels credible rather than promotional.
- Use small 13–15px quote text and enough line height for readability; allow horizontal scrolling or a stacked layout on mobile.
- Keep the strip visually quiet and separate it from neighboring sections with whitespace rather than heavy borders.

Never:
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never invent endorsements for real organizations.
