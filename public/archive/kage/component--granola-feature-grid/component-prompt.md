## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than copying the reference.

Build a benefits section with a large serif section heading at the top and three horizontally separated rows beneath it. Each row should have a small line icon or abstract glyph at left and one concise benefit sentence at right. Use generous vertical spacing, thin light-gray rules (#ecece6), a white/off-white background, and a clear left alignment grid. Heading typography should be editorial serif at roughly 42–56px; row copy should be 13–15px sans-serif. Keep icons monochrome and functional, with subtle hover emphasis on the row.

Never:
- use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference
- duplicate the reference’s benefit wording or icons
- turn the section into a dense card grid
