## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a feature CTA band:
- Use a soft pastel gradient background and a two-column layout with copy on the left and a product artifact on the right.
- Include a small eyebrow, concise bold heading, 2–3 lines of explanatory text, and a dark filled CTA.
- Treat the visual as a pair of overlapping cards or badges with modest rotation, shadows, and generous negative space.
- Use a 28–32px heading, 16–18px body copy, 14px eyebrow, and a button with 14–16px label.
- On mobile, stack the copy first and center the visual beneath it; preserve the artifact's readable scale.

Never:
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never reuse ticket designs or exact promotional language.
