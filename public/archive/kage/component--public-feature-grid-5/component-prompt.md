## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Build a dark trust-and-security section. Begin with a split hero row: a large serif two-line trust statement on the left and an abstract, nonliteral technology visual on the right. Under it, use a three-column grid over two rows, where every item has a concise heading, two or three lines of proof-oriented copy, and optional small compliance chips. Use black (#050505), warm white (#F2F2ED), subdued gray text (#929292), #202020 dividers, and one cool blue accent. Keep the visual subdued and avoid decorative clutter; the content should communicate regulation, coverage, transparency, infrastructure, and uptime with clear hierarchy.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
