## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to their product rather than copying this reference.

Create a full-width closing CTA with a very subtle dark-green atmospheric background. Center a small eyebrow, a large two-line invitation, one sentence of reassurance, two horizontally aligned buttons, and a tiny note about starting cost or commitment. Use a spacious 280–360px band, #07100d to #07090a gradient, primary text #edf2ef, muted text #91a099, mint-white primary button #e9f5ed with dark text, and transparent secondary button with #28322e border. Use 10–12px button radius and 16px section radius only if the CTA is contained. Keep the hierarchy clear and avoid adding extra navigation or feature detail. Buttons should have hover contrast and visible focus states; stack them on small screens.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
