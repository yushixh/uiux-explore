## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Build a compact desktop-first navigation bar for a modern financial or software product. Use a white background, a small brand mark area at left, three or four centered text links with restrained chevrons, and two right-aligned account actions: a dark filled primary button and a light outlined secondary button. Keep the bar about 56–64px tall, align everything to one horizontal baseline, use generous side padding, and preserve a clear mobile collapse behavior. Use near-black text (#111111), white (#FFFFFF), a thin pale border (#E9E9E9), 999px button radius, and a neutral sans-serif at 12–14px. Make hover states change text opacity and button fill subtly.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
