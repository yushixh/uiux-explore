## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Build a horizontal credibility strip on a warm off-white background. Center a compact review signal alongside several monochrome publication or partner marks, separated by generous whitespace rather than heavy dividers. Keep the strip short, calm, and secondary to the hero; use small sans-serif metadata and subdued gray-black marks. Add responsive wrapping or horizontal scrolling on narrow screens. Use `#f8f7f3`, `#222222`, and muted gray `#a5a5a0`, with no card shadows unless needed for contrast.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
