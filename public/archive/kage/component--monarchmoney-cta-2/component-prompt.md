## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Create a full-width closing CTA inside a rounded rectangle with a vivid orange or brand-primary background `#ff672f`. Center a large light serif headline across two or three lines and place a white pill button beneath it. Use light cream text `#fff8ef`, generous vertical padding, and a 14–18px radius. Keep the message short and emotionally positive, with a single unmistakable action. On mobile, reduce the headline size and preserve the same centered hierarchy.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
