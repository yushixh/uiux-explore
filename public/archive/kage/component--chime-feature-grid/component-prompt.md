## Before you start
Ask what product feature should be highlighted, what supporting features exist, and what visual assets are available in the user's brand. Then design a reusable feature showcase.

Create a large rounded feature panel on a deep green page. Use a two-column interior: left for a section title, three short checkmarked benefits, a secondary outline button, and a subdued vertical list of other feature names; right for a large rounded-corner lifestyle or product image with a small overlay label. Give the panel generous internal padding around 24–32px, a 12–18px radius, and a slightly lighter green surface around #07543A against a #003B2B page. Use large 26–32px white headings, 14–16px body copy, mint checkmarks, and an outline button with a low-contrast border. Treat the left list as navigation: the active item is bright, inactive items are low-opacity, and clicking an item changes the content and image. Maintain a balanced 42/58 or 45/55 split and stack cleanly on mobile.

Never: copy the reference's feature names, text, imagery, logo, product UI, brand marks, or exact layout artwork.
