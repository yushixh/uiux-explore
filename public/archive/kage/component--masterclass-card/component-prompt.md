## Before you start
Ask what the user's adjacent products, audiences, and brand are, then create two related promotional cards for them.

Build two vertically stacked promotional panels on a black page. The first is a wide split card with a dark olive text half, a refined display headline, short proof statement, bright lime/yellow CTA, and a warm photographic certificate half. The second is a dark charcoal business card with an overlapping or offset lifestyle image on the left and a text/action block on the right. Use 16px outer rounding, cropped imagery, high contrast, generous internal padding, and consistent alignment. Palette: #17180d or #242629 surfaces, ivory text, muted gray copy, warm amber imagery, and an energetic lime accent for the certificate action; use hot pink sparingly for business conversion. On mobile, stack the image and text while preserving clear CTA order.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
