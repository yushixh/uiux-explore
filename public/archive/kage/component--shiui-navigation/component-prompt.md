## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a thin global navigation for a design-system or product catalogue. Use a warm off-white background, hairline gray borders, a small brand lockup at left, compact uppercase navigation links, and right-aligned utility buttons. Keep the header around 56–64px tall, use a narrow sans-serif at 9–11px with generous letter spacing, and make the active/action button dark charcoal with a subtle squared radius. Align everything to the same centered content grid used by the page. Add clear hover/focus states and a responsive collapse for narrow screens.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations or imagery from the reference.
