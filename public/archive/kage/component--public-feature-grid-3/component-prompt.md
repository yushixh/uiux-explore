## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Design a dark feature band for an advanced trading or developer product. Center a small eyebrow and a large two-line serif statement at the top, then place three equal-width columns beneath it. Each column should have a short bold title, two or three lines of explanatory copy, a small text link, and a different dark UI panel or data visualization below. Use black (#050505), off-white text (#F5F5F1), muted gray copy (#9A9A9A), near-black panel borders (#202020), subtle blue/green accents, and 1px dividers. Use 16px internal padding and modest 8–12px panel radii. Keep the composition premium and information-dense without making the cards feel like a conventional pricing table.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
