## Before you start
Ask what problem motivated the user's product, what belief the brand should communicate, and whether they have an abstract symbol or visual motif. Apply the structure to that story.

Create a dark split-layout story section. Place a large rounded visual or typographic motif in the left third, and an eyebrow, oversized serif heading, two short paragraphs, and a bordered pull quote in the right two-thirds. Use a near-black charcoal background #202123, warm white #f0eee8, muted grey #aaa8a2, and an accent red #bf332b. Add a faint geometric texture or gradient only if it supports the brand. Use 24–32px outer padding, 32–48px column gap, 14–18px card radius, and a 2px accent rule beside the quote. Maintain readable line lengths and stack the columns on mobile.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
