## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, destinations, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Create a destination discovery carousel on a very light warm-gray background (#f8f8fa). Use a max-width container with a bold 18–20px heading and right-aligned circular previous/next controls. Below, show 3–4 large landscape cards in a horizontal track, each around 235px wide and 155px tall with 24px radius, scenic imagery, a bottom gradient, a large white destination label, and a thin coral accent line at the bottom. Let the next card peek into view to communicate horizontal scroll. On mobile, show one full card plus the next partial card. Support keyboard controls, touch dragging, and visible focus rings.

Never: copy logos, product names, exact copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
