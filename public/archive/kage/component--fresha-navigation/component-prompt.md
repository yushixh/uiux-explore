## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to that context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a responsive global navigation bar. Use a white background, a subtle bottom border, a compact brand mark or wordmark on the left, and a right-aligned cluster of low-emphasis text links plus rounded outlined actions. Keep the header around 52–72px tall, horizontally padded, vertically centered, and visually calm. Use near-black text (`#111111`), pale gray borders (`#e5e5e5`), 14–16px typography, and pill radii around 24px. Collapse secondary actions into a menu on small screens while preserving the primary action.

Never: use the reference's logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or branding.
