## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that context rather than reproducing a restaurant-booking site.

Build a low-pressure discovery CTA that helps users start an AI or guided search. Follow these rules:
- Use a wide, shallow panel with a very subtle cream-to-aqua gradient such as #fff5ed to #e9fbf6, with generous 32px internal spacing.
- Arrange a compact text block on the left and a flexible cluster of suggested prompt chips on the right; stack them on mobile.
- Use a 20px semibold heading, 13px supporting text, and a tiny muted attribution/status line. Keep copy short and outcome-oriented.
- Render suggestions as white or near-white pills/cards with #d9dddf borders, 4–6px radius, 12–13px text, and clear hover/focus treatment.
- Ensure the whole component feels optional: no aggressive color, no modal interruption, and no excessive visual noise.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, imagery, or exact visual assets from the reference.
