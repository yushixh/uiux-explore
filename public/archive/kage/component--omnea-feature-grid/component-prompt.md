## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand personality and primary conversion goal are. Then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a platform overview section with a small eyebrow, centered headline, and concise supporting sentence. Under it, show a lightweight conceptual diagram or orbit of colored nodes representing product areas, followed by four equal-width cards. Each card should have a short category title, one-sentence explanation, and a compact list of linked capabilities with right arrows. Use warm white #fbfbfa, muted charcoal #242624, pale gray #eeeeeb borders, and restrained accent dots in soft green, blue, coral, and lavender. Cards should use 1px borders, 2–6px radius, 20–24px padding, and equal heights. Finish with a quiet full-width strip that reinforces the platform architecture. Use a 4-column grid on desktop and a 1-column stack on mobile.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
