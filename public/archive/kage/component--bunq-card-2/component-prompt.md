## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the following principles to that context.

Create a horizontally browsable feature-card carousel for a mobile product landing page. Center a strong white section heading with a two-line supporting description above the carousel. Each card should have a large rounded lifestyle image, a dark charcoal caption panel, concise white title and muted body copy, and one full-width pill CTA. Show a small slice of the next card at the right edge to signal swipeability, while keeping the active card dominant. Use 12px page gutters, 16px radius, black background, subtle charcoal surfaces (#171717), and scroll-snap behavior. Add accessible previous/next controls or keyboard support even if they are visually minimal.

### Never
- Never use the reference logo, product name, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
- Never copy the exact card image, subject, or wording.
- Never hide carousel affordances from keyboard and screen-reader users.
