## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Build a full-width closing CTA over a rich, darkened lifestyle image or video still. Center a compact white card with generous rounded corners, a small neutral brand mark or icon placeholder, a concise invitation, one prominent dark pill button, and a secondary sign-in link. Keep the card highly legible and conversion-focused, with optional app-store or secondary destination badges beneath. Use responsive sizing so the card remains comfortably padded and never dominates the visual on desktop or mobile.

Never: use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
