## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a feature grid with a short section introduction and four equally weighted cards in a 2×2 desktop arrangement. Each card should have a softly rounded visual preview at the top, a concise benefit title, and one or two lines explaining the outcome. Use varied but coherent abstract UI previews rather than decorative clutter. Finish the grid with a centered testimonial quote and attribution. Use a dark #171721 background, #23232f card surfaces, 1px #343543 borders, 10px radius, 20–28px card padding, 14px labels, and 18–24px titles. Collapse to one column on mobile while preserving visual rhythm.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
