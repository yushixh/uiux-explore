## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a testimonial section:
- Use a wide, airy layout with a left editorial column and a right masonry-like grid of review cards.
- Put a compact rating badge above a 36–44px two-line heading, followed by a short explanation and one CTA.
- Make review cards white with a very light border, small radius around 8px, subtle shadow, compact metadata, and readable 12–14px text.
- Keep the review wall varied but aligned; on mobile, convert it to a scrollable list or two-column grid.
- Use authentic-looking structure—rating, title, date, reviewer, and body—without fabricating endorsements.

Never:
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never claim reviews are real unless supplied by the product owner.
