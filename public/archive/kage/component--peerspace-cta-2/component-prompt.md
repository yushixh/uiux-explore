## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then tailor this secondary-audience acquisition section to the user's business.

Build a near-black split-layout CTA with a vivid portrait or lifestyle image on the left and concise white copy on the right. Use a strong two-line heading, a short supporting statement, one explanatory paragraph, and a contrasting light button. Keep the image in a fixed editorial crop, align content vertically to its center, and use 40–56px heading typography, 16px body text, 24–40px internal spacing, and an 8px button radius. Stack image above copy on mobile and ensure the CTA is the dominant focus.

Never: use the reference logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or distinctive brand assets.
