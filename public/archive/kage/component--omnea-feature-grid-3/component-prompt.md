## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand personality and primary conversion goal are. Then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create an enterprise trust section with a split heading row: an eyebrow and large serif headline on the left, a short reassurance paragraph on the right. Under it, show three equal cards describing control, model/data policy, and traceability. Each card should include a very light geometric diagram made with CSS or inline SVG, a bold principle title, and two lines of explanation. Follow with a full-width trust strip containing five small compliance/governance categories in cells. Use #fbfbfa, #eeeeeb dividers, pale green accent points, and diagram lines in #cfd5cf. Favor 1px borders and 4px corners; keep diagrams secondary to the copy and make the layout responsive.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
