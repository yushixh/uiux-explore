## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a multi-column footer on a pale neutral background around #F4F4F1. Use a thin top border and three main zones: an about link column, a support/resources column, and a brand/app/account column. Head each group with 12–14px semibold text, then use small muted links with comfortable vertical spacing. Include a prominent dark pill CTA, optional app-store badge, and a compact row of social icons. Finish with a separate legal bar divided by a horizontal rule, containing privacy/terms links, accessibility or trust marks, and a right-aligned copyright line. Ensure strong contrast, semantic footer landmarks, and a stacked responsive layout.

Never:
- copy logos, product names, or reference copy
- reuse reference imagery or illustrations
- reproduce the exact brand styling
