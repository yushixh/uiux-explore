## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Create an immersive full-bleed testimonial band using a close-up, brand-relevant portrait or video still with a subtle dark gradient. Place a short, large quote near the bottom edge and the speaker attribution aligned to the opposite side. Keep the treatment editorial: sparse UI, bright high-contrast type, ample breathing room, and enough overlay for legibility. If the testimonial is part of a carousel, expose discreet controls without competing with the quote.

Never: use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
