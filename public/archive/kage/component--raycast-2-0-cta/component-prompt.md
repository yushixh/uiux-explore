## Before you start
Ask what the user's final conversion goal, CTA label, product promise, and compatibility constraints are. Apply these principles to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Create a tall, centered closing CTA on a pure black background. Use a restrained two-line headline around 25px, with the first line in muted #9b9b9f and the emphasized line in #f1f1f2. Place a light neutral primary button (#f0f0f0) with dark text below, then a tiny muted compatibility note with generous spacing. Add a very subtle abstract product/device visual or layered geometric treatment in the lower portion, fading into the background via a black gradient; it should support the CTA without competing with it. Keep the composition vertically spacious and symmetric. The button needs a clear hover state and should remain prominent on mobile.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
