## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Design a premium eligibility CTA on a black background. Use a two-column layout: the left side contains a small eyebrow, an elegant large serif question or proposition, and a short supporting paragraph; the right side contains a subtle bordered panel with a short benefits label, three checked benefits, and an outlined or filled action button. Use #050505 background, #F5F5F0 heading text, #A0A0A0 body text, #202020 borders, small green or blue check accents, and 12–16px radii. Keep the panel narrower than the copy column and align its vertical center to the headline. On mobile, stack the panel underneath.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
