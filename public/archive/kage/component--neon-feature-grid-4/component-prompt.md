## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to their identity rather than reproducing this reference.

Design a spacious dark feature band. Align a small dotted accent and a large text block within a centered content column, with a 42–50px first sentence in white and the explanatory continuation in muted gray. Let negative space do much of the work; do not fill the section with unnecessary cards. Optionally include one restrained inline action or link beneath the statement. Use #000, #f4f4ef, #747a78, and #36d5a0, with a thin divider at the top and bottom and 1.2–1.3 line-height for the headline.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
