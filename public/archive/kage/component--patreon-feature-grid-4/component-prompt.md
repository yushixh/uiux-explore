## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Create a business-growth feature section with a large centered or slightly offset headline at the top. Below it, arrange a dark analytics/product mockup beside two short benefit columns, each with a small heading, compact paragraph, and optional pill CTA. Use the same airy blue surface as the surrounding story, strong black typography, rounded controls, and a clear hierarchy from promise to evidence to action. Make the data mockup illustrative and responsive rather than dependent on exact screenshots.

Never: use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
