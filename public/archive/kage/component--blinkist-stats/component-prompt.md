## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a compact social-proof band with a centered testimonial quote at the top, a small star-rating treatment and attribution beneath it, and three equal-width metrics below. Each metric should use a simple blue line icon, a large bold number, and a smaller explanatory label. Keep the background white and the visual rhythm calm: 32–48px vertical padding, thin or invisible dividers, navy #073544 for headings, blue #1677c8 for icons, and muted #5f6869 for supporting text. Ensure the layout collapses to a vertical list on narrow screens and that the quote remains readable.

Never use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
