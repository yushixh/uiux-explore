## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a format-selection section with a centered headline and one-line explanation. Below it, show an intentionally oversized horizontal card carousel where the active card is fully visible and adjacent cards peek in from both sides to invite swiping. Use a soft lavender active card, rounded 24px corners, editorial serif or display accents inside the card, concise copy, and a collage-like product/content visual on the right. Add pagination bars and chevron controls below. Use navy #073544, lavender #e9e4ff, warm cream #fff4d8, purple #6849c8, and restrained dark text. Keep the active card accessible, draggable or swipeable, and responsive.

Never use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
