## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to their identity rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a black multi-column footer with a max-width container. Put a simple brand lockup and legal/copyright text in the first column, then four compact link columns with uppercase 9–10px headings and 12–13px muted links. Add a final row for privacy, terms, accessibility, and status links. Use #000, #f4f5f0, #7c8380, #252a28, and a restrained green accent. Keep line-height generous enough for scanning, make every link keyboard accessible, and collapse into accordions or a stacked layout on mobile.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
