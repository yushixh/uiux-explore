## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to their product rather than copying this reference.

Design a feature section about parallel work orchestration. Use a two-column intro with an eyebrow, a large two-line headline, and a short explanation with a small text link. Below, create a wide dark diagram card with a central coordinator node, three peer worker nodes, and a reviewer node connected by thin routed lines. Add three evenly spaced numbered takeaways beneath the diagram. Use a near-black canvas #07090a, a slightly lighter card #0d1112, borders #252b29, 14–18px radii, fine connector lines #39433f, mint highlights #a9efc3, and subtle blue or amber node accents. Typography should contrast a large 42–56px headline with 11px labels and 13–15px explanatory text. The diagram should remain understandable when stacked or simplified on mobile, and nodes may reveal hover details.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
