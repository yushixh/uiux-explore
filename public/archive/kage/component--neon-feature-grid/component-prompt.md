## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to their identity rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a dark feature section with a narrow left rail of category links and a larger right content column. Lead with a large 42–52px statement where the first phrase is bright and the supporting phrase is muted. Under it, show a framed product/terminal visualization offset toward the right, with subtle grid texture, a dark panel, monospace text, and one bright accent. Add a compact integration row beneath the visual. Use #000, #101514 panels, #2a302e borders, #f3f4ef primary text, #737977 secondary text, and #39d5a1 accent. Give the active rail item a small indicator, make categories interactive, and keep the visual below the heading at desktop while stacking it on mobile.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
