## Before you start
Ask which legal, support, and utility destinations the user's product needs. Use those real destinations and the user's brand voice.

Build a minimal footer on the same pale background as the closing CTA. Center a single row of small utility links, place a bold product or company label beneath it, and add a very small muted disclaimer at the bottom. Keep it visually quiet and avoid adding another heavy navigation system.

Design language: background #efffd5, text #586151, primary footer label #11150d, and fine print #a1aa99. Use 12–13px links with 20–28px gaps, 13px bold brand label, and 10–11px disclaimer text. Use 40–60px top/bottom padding, no large borders, and underline or color-shift links on hover. Stack and center links on mobile while preserving comfortable tap targets.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
