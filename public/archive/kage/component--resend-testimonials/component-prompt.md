## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand voice and visual identity are. Apply the following principles to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Design a minimal centered testimonial band. Place a small neutral avatar, monogram, or abstract customer mark above a large readable quote, then show the speaker's name and role in subdued text. Use a black background, white quote text around 20–26px, gray attribution at 12–14px, and generous vertical spacing. Keep the quote width constrained to roughly 420–520px for emphasis. If multiple quotes exist, use accessible previous/next controls or pagination dots, but avoid distracting motion.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
