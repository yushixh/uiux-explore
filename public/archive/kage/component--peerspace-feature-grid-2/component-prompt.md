## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then adapt the benefit story to their actual differentiators.

Build a light editorial benefits section with an oversized two-line heading at the top, partially overlapping or visually connecting to the content below. Under it, use a two-column carousel: a large landscape image on the left and a concise benefit heading, paragraph, arrow controls, and slide counter on the right. Use black text on white, strong 64–96px display type for the title, 28–36px benefit heading, generous whitespace, and a thin understated control treatment. Make the carousel swipeable, keyboard accessible, and responsive with image-first stacking on mobile.

Never: use the reference logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or distinctive brand assets.
