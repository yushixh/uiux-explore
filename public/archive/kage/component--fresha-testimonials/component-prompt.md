## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to that context rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a testimonial carousel on a white background with a left-aligned heading and four equal cards. Give cards a very light gray fill (`#f5f5f5`), 14–18px radius, generous internal padding, and enough height for the reviewer identity to sit near the bottom. Show a warm yellow star row, a bold review title, readable quote text, and a circular avatar with name and location. Use 16px gaps, an edge arrow, and horizontal scrolling on mobile. Keep the tone editorial and trustworthy without adding excessive decoration.

Never: use the reference's logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or branding.
