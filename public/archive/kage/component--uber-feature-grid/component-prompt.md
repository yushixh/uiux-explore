## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a section with a prominent left-aligned heading and a responsive 3-column grid of six service cards. Each card should have a soft neutral background around `#f5f5f5`, 16–20px internal padding, a concise title, two lines of supporting text, a small text link, and a visual anchored toward the lower-right or right edge. Keep cards equal height with 12px corner radii and a 16px grid gap. Use a 30–36px section heading, 14–16px descriptions, and strong contrast for titles. On tablet and mobile, reduce to two and one columns respectively while preserving the card order. Add subtle hover elevation or background change and ensure the detail link has a clear focus state.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
