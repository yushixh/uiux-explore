## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, company, jurisdictions, and required legal links are, then build an original footer around those requirements.

Build a spacious white footer with a max-width content container. Put the brand treatment at top, followed by a row of primary support/company links and a second row of legal and accessibility links separated by a thin #D9D9D9 divider. Below, include a readable company/licensing statement and a dense but well-spaced legal disclosure area with superscript references and linked terms. Use 14px utility links, 12–13px legal text, #111111 text, muted #555555 secondary text, and a bright blue accent near #1683D8. Use 64px top padding, 32px internal gaps, and stack all link groups cleanly on mobile. Maintain adequate line height and do not hide essential legal text behind tiny overflow containers.

### Never
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never copy the reference's legal wording or licensing claims.
