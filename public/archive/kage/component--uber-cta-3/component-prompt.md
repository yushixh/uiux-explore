## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a business-focused split callout with text on the left and an editorial visual on the right. Use a large two-line heading, one concise value proposition paragraph, a high-contrast primary button, and a secondary text link. Align the copy vertically around the center of the visual, use a 40–48px heading and 16px body text, and provide 80–120px of breathing room around the row. Keep the image in a 12–16px rounded rectangle with `object-fit: cover`; use a restrained white/black palette and let the product-specific accent appear only in links or focus states. Stack on mobile and retain action hierarchy. Include hover, focus, and reduced-motion-friendly transitions.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
