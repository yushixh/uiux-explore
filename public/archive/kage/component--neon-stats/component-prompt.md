## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to their identity rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a pale mint editorial proof section using a two-column layout with a thin vertical divider. In the left column place a tiny uppercase eyebrow, a large 38–48px credibility statement, and two bottom-aligned stats with oversized numerals and short labels. In the right column place a tiny trust eyebrow, a monospace or typewriter-style quote, and attribution. Use #e6f3ee, #111917, #65716d, #df5b62 for small markers, and #aabcb5 dividers. On mobile, stack the columns while preserving the stat hierarchy. Keep the tone factual and let whitespace signal confidence.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
