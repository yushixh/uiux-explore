## Before you start
Ask what customer stories and verified outcomes the user's product can share, then build an original carousel from that material.

Create a testimonial carousel section with a centered serif heading and three visible story cards on desktop. Emphasize the active center card with greater scale, opacity, and visual prominence; allow adjacent cards to peek in at the edges to invite browsing.

Design rules:
- Layout: full-width carousel with a max-width around 900px; center card width 330–380px, side cards slightly narrower or partially clipped.
- Cards: white backgrounds, 12–16px radius, thin #DFDFDF border, soft shadow; image at top with consistent aspect ratio.
- Typography: story quote in 15–18px sans-serif, customer name and role at bottom in 12–14px; serif section heading 32–42px.
- Accent: subtle blurred colour trails or gradients behind active card using the user's palette; never reduce text contrast.
- Controls: dots and previous/next buttons, keyboard navigation, touch swipe, and visible active state.
- Responsive: show one card at a time on mobile with readable controls.

Never:
- Never reuse reference photos, quotes, names, logos, product name, or claims.
- Never autoplay rapidly or hide carousel controls.
- Never crop faces or important visual content.
