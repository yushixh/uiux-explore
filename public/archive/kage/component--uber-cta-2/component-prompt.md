## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a two-column partner/recruitment callout with an image or product visual on the left and text on the right. Give the visual a substantial 16px radius and a fixed aspect ratio; keep the copy vertically centered with a 40–48px heading, a short paragraph, a primary dark button, and a small sign-in or existing-user link. Use ample whitespace, a max-width around 1200px, 48–96px vertical padding, and a 48–64px column gap. Keep the palette mostly white with near-black text and a single dark action color. On mobile, stack visual first or follow the user's conversion priority, make the button full-width when useful, and preserve a strong focus ring.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
