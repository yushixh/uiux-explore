## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a simple outcomes band with three equal columns. Use very large bold percentage figures, concise explanatory labels underneath, and a small centered footnote explaining the source or methodology. Keep the composition spacious and credible, with no cards or heavy borders. Use navy #073544 for figures, dark gray #4f5b5c for labels, white background, 48–72px desktop spacing, and responsive stacking. Make the footnote visibly secondary but readable and avoid implying unsupported claims.

Never use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
