## Before you start
Ask what two capabilities are most important for the user's product and what visual atmosphere fits its brand. Then apply the structure below with original content.

Build a two-column feature introduction over a full-bleed atmospheric background image, texture, or gradient. Add a centered panel with a subtle translucent dark-green surface, a fine light border, and generous internal padding. Each column should have a simple line icon above a concise two-line heading and a short mono or plain-text explanation. Keep the columns equal width, separated by whitespace rather than a heavy divider, and align icon, heading, and body consistently. Use deep teal/forest tones (#164a3f, #0d3e39), misted cream text (#f6f5e8), and a low-opacity white border. Use 20–25px headings, 11–13px supporting copy, 30–40px panel padding, and a soft blur/overlay so text remains readable. On mobile, stack the columns while preserving the same hierarchy.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
