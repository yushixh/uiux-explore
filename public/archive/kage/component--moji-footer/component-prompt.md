## Before you start
Ask what legal, attribution, support, and social links the user's product requires, and what compact footer treatment fits the brand.

Create a slim footer with a top border and a centered max-width row. Put a compact product mark and one-line descriptor on the left, a small muted copyright or license statement in the center, and a circular back-to-top button on the right. Use an off-white background #faf9f5, charcoal #202123, muted grey #8d8a83, and a very subtle border #e5e2db. Keep type small but readable, use generous horizontal padding, and provide hover/focus states for the utility control. Collapse to a stacked two-row layout on mobile while preserving the brand first.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
