## Before you start
Ask what final action the user's product wants visitors to take and what brand visual should make that action feel aspirational. Use the following layout principles with new content.

Create a final two-column CTA on a white or warm-white surface. Put a large product visual or abstract brand object on the left, allowing it to breathe and partially overlap the section grid. On the right, place a framed content panel with a large serif headline, one or two lines of supporting copy, a filled primary button, and an outlined secondary button. Use a 55/45 visual-to-copy split, thin pale gray rules, square corner brackets, and generous vertical whitespace. Keep the palette near-white (#fbfbf7), ink (#172021), forest green (#0d6a50), and pale lime (#d7ed86). Use 34–48px heading type, 12–14px support copy, and 4–6px button radius. Preserve the asymmetry on desktop and stack image above copy on mobile.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
