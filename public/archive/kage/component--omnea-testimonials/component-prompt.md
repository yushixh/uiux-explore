## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand personality and primary conversion goal are. Then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a customer stories section with a left-aligned heading and a horizontally scrollable row of four testimonial cards. Give each card a distinct quiet background or portrait treatment, short quote, and compact person/role attribution; keep text readable over imagery with a soft gradient if necessary. Beneath the cards, add a simple line introducing aggregate results and three large percentages with concise descriptions. Use warm white #fbfbfa, cards with 1px #e5e5e0 borders or muted image backgrounds, 4–8px radius, and generous 24px internal padding. On desktop show four cards with partial overflow or carousel controls; on mobile use snap scrolling and preserve accessible keyboard navigation.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
