## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to that context rather than reproducing this reference.

Design a generous app-promotion CTA with a light neutral or white background. Place a small availability/platform row above an oversized two-line heading, followed by concise supporting copy and a download action. Use a two-column composition on desktop: content aligned left in a max-width of about 360px and a product-preview area on the right; allow the preview to be a neutral placeholder until real product assets exist. Use 48–80px section padding, 48–64px heading typography, black text, and a soft rounded device/mockup treatment. Stack the columns on mobile.

Never: use the reference's logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or branding.
