## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than copying the reference.

Build a dark testimonial section with a centered serif heading and a two- or three-card horizontal layout. Each card should use a thin muted border (#77746d), generous padding, a compact customer/category label, a large readable quote in a serif face, and an attribution row with a small avatar placeholder and role. Use charcoal (#292929), warm white text (#f5f3eb), and muted secondary text. Cards may overflow horizontally on mobile as a scrollable carousel, with keyboard-accessible controls and clear focus states.

Never:
- use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference
- use the reference testimonials, people, or portraits
- make the quote text too small to read comfortably
