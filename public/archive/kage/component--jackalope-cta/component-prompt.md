## Before you start
Ask what conversion action the user's product needs, what incentive or promise should accompany it, and what legal consent text is required. Build the CTA around that information.

Create a full-width high-contrast signup CTA with a large white heading on the left, a short supporting sentence, and an inline email form containing a wide input and compact submit button. Add a small consent line and an optional checkbox beneath. Use a saturated indigo background near #5949ef, white type, a white input surface, and a slightly darker or white-outlined button. Use 48–64px heading typography, 14px supporting text, 12px input/button radii, 16px form gap, and generous horizontal/vertical padding. Include clear validation, loading, success, and error states, and stack the form vertically on small screens.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
