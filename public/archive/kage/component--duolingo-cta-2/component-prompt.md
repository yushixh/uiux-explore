## Before you start
Ask what final action the user's product wants visitors to take and what emotional tone should close the page, then apply the principles below to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a centered closing CTA with a short two-line headline, one prominent primary button, and a playful abstract background or low-contrast product motif extending toward the bottom. Use a white upper area transitioning into a bright brand-colored lower field, approximately #58CC02, with contrasting decorative accents. Keep the headline dark or green, use a wide rounded primary button, and reserve enough vertical space for the visual to feel celebratory without reducing clarity. Ensure the action remains visible and accessible on mobile.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
