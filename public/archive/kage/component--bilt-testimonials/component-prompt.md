## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are. Then create credible proof appropriate to that product.

Build a testimonial section with a concise heading and optional carousel controls aligned to the right. Below it, show three equal dark quote cards. Each card should include a small source/category label and a short, highly legible quote; keep the cards visually quiet so the words carry the authority.

Design language: #020b15 page background, cards around #07131e or #0d1b26, off-white quote text #edf2f4, muted source labels #a7b3bc, 12px radius, and 1px rgba(255,255,255,.06) borders. Use 24px gaps, 28–32px card padding, 16–18px quote type, and 72px vertical padding. On mobile, use a one-card carousel or stacked cards. Controls should be accessible and never be the only way to discover additional testimonials.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
