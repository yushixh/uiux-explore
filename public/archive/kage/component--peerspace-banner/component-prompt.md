## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to their own campaign or announcement.

Build a full-width promotional banner with a saturated dark-purple background around #28143D. Use an asymmetrical two-column composition: a layered collage of three or more rectangular images on the left and a compact announcement lockup on the right. Make the campaign title the visual focus with condensed or heavy display typography, a small eyebrow above it, and a lightweight arrow link beneath. Keep image edges crisp, avoid excessive card chrome, and use responsive stacking on narrow screens. Allow the imagery and type to feel editorial and oversized while retaining a clear accessible CTA.

Never: use the reference logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or distinctive brand assets.
