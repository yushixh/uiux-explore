## Before you start
Ask who the user's most credible customer groups are and what authentic outcomes they can verify. Use real, permissioned proof or clearly labeled placeholders.

Build a dark social-proof section with a near-black background (`#171815`) and rounded outer corners around 28–36px. Center a large cream serif headline, followed by a muted supporting sentence. Below, stack compact testimonial cards in a single readable column; use charcoal surfaces (`#242521`), subtle 1px borders (`#3b3b35`), 14–16px radii, a small avatar placeholder or initials, author name/role, and a short italic or regular quote. Keep each card approximately 56–80px tall so a large collection feels like a community wall rather than a few oversized case studies. End the section with a bordered inset panel containing a concise reassurance question and several small lime or pale-green action pills. Preserve strong contrast and generous outer padding.

Never:
- use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference
- invent customer identities, quotes, or outcomes
- use unlicensed headshots
