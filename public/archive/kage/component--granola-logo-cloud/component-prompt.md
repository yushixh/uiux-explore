## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than copying the reference.

Create a full-width dark trust section using a charcoal background (#292929) and a centered small heading in warm white (#f4f3ec). Arrange recognizable customer marks in two evenly spaced rows with generous gutters and consistent visual heights. Treat marks as monochrome white or light gray so the section feels cohesive; include faint separators or decorative plus signs only if they support the grid. Use a compact sans-serif eyebrow and preserve substantial top and bottom padding. On hover, allow a logo to brighten slightly without changing layout.

Never:
- use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference
- include the reference’s customer names or exact logo arrangement
- use bright multicolour marks against the dark background
