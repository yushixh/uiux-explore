## Before you start
Ask what the user's product captures, organizes, or automates, and what proof would make that benefit credible. Build an original section around those answers.

Design an editorial feature section with a small numbered eyebrow, a bold headline with an italic accent, and a short explanatory paragraph. Place a large centered device or interface demonstration above or beside the copy, but use an original UI appropriate to the user's product. Beneath it, use three equal white cards with short labels, compact titles, and two-to-three sentence explanations. Keep the layout within a centered max-width, use a warm `#f5efe3` background, white cards `#fffdf8`, text `#171714`, muted text `#686257`, and a small amber accent `#d3971b`. Use 18–24px card radii, subtle shadows or 1px borders, 24px gaps, and clear responsive stacking. The section should explain the value before asking the user to act.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
