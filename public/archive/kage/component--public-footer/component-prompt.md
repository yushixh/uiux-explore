## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Build a comprehensive responsive footer. Start with a white upper footer containing four or five columns: product links, resources, quick links, contact details, and a brand/app-download area. Add generous column gaps, 12–14px sans-serif links, compact headings, and subtle hover underlines. Below, add a pale blue-gray legal region (#F3F8FA) with a row of policy links, copyright, and long-form regulatory disclosures in 10–11px muted text. Use #111111 headings, #555555 body text, #DDE5E8 separators, 24–48px padding, and a clear max-width grid. Collapse columns into accordions on mobile while keeping critical disclosures readable.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
