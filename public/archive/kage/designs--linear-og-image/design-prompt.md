# 1200×630 social card — centered wordmark on dark gradient

## Before you start
Ask the user what brand this card is for, who it targets, and what assets they have (logo mark, wordmark, brand colours, typeface). Wait for answers; use placeholder branding until then.

## Output
Produce a single 1200×630 image, e.g. an HTML/CSS file rendered to PNG or a standalone SVG. It is an Open Graph/social share card.

## Page structure (canvas composition)
- Full-bleed canvas: near-black background (#0a0a0a) with a subtle vertical/radial gradient that brightens to a soft grey haze (#3a3a3a–#555) toward the bottom edge — like light rising from below.
- Exact center of the canvas: one horizontal brand lockup — a simple geometric logo mark (e.g. a solid circle or square with 2–3 thin diagonal negative-space slits) in pure white, roughly 90px tall, followed by ~40px gap, then the wordmark in white, bold sans-serif, ~110px cap height, set in Title Case.
- Nothing else: no headline, tagline, URL or imagery. All elements must fit comfortably within the middle third so safe zones survive cropping in Slack/X previews.

## Design language
- Monochrome only: pure white (#fff) artwork on near-black (#0a0a0a); depth comes from the gradient, not colour.
- One focal element: the lockup IS the hierarchy — huge scale contrast against an empty field.
- Use a single geometric sans (Inter/Neue Haas style), medium-to-bold weight, tight tracking (-2%).
- No borders, shadows or texture beyond the gradient glow.

## Never
- Do not use the Linear name, logo mark, or any copy from the reference; use the user's own or placeholder branding. Never present the result as Linear's card.
