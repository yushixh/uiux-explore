## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to that context rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a proof-points section with a small centered lead-in and three evenly distributed statistic columns. Use large bold numerals around 32–44px, short captions beneath, and generous horizontal spacing. Keep the section visually minimal on white, with no card borders unless needed for accessibility. On mobile, stack the metrics or use a two-column layout while preserving clear scanability. Use near-black text and one restrained accent only if it supports emphasis.

Never: use the reference's logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or branding.
