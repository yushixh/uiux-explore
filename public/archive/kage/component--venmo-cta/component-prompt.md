## Before you start
Ask what campaign, offer, audience, and brand colors the user's product needs, then adapt this promotional pattern accordingly.

Create a full-width promotional CTA band with a saturated indigo-violet background near #4337F2. Center a large white headline, a short explanatory paragraph, a small terms line, and a contrasting white pill button. Place a wide rounded media panel beneath the copy, using an original product image or mockup that extends the campaign story. Use 36–44px heading type, 14–16px body copy, 24px panel radius, and high-contrast white controls. Keep the content centered with a max width around 700px, use 72–96px vertical padding, and ensure the media crops gracefully on mobile. Add a subtle hover lift to the CTA and visible focus styles.

### Never
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never reuse the reference reward name, screenshot, or purple treatment as a brand imitation.
