## Before you start
Ask what the user's primary conversion is, what value users receive immediately, and what brand mood should surround the final invitation. Apply those answers to the CTA.

Create a full-width final CTA band with a softly tinted background and centered content. Use a concise serif heading, two or three lines of supporting copy, and one dark primary button. Keep decorative elements optional and peripheral so they never compete with the conversion action. Make the section feel like a calm conclusion to the page, with generous vertical padding and a clear contrast change from the preceding content.

Use background `#f5f5f5`, near-black `#080808`, muted gray `#5e5e5e`, a black button with white text, 8px radius, 16px horizontal padding, and 28–36px heading size. Include hover, focus, and loading states for the CTA.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
