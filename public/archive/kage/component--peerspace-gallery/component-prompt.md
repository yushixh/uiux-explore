## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then translate this editorial gallery idea to their content.

Build a dark full-width gallery with an oversized white display title spanning behind or around a horizontal row of tightly cropped portrait images. Let the image strip cross the title visually, use left and right arrow controls at the edges, and place a small descriptive label plus light CTA toward the lower right. Use #111111 and #FFFFFF with image-led color accents, minimal borders, and consistent rectangular crops. The row should support drag, keyboard navigation, and touch scrolling; on mobile show fewer cards while keeping the oversized title legible.

Never: use the reference logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or distinctive brand assets.
