## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a spacious editorial statement section on an off-white background. Use a two-part composition: a small eyebrow and oversized serif statement on the left/top, followed by a lower row with a short explanatory paragraph and one dark rounded CTA aligned toward the right. Add an optional abstract hand-drawn underline, circle, or arrow as a brand accent, but keep it decorative and lightweight. Use generous vertical whitespace, a strong serif/sans contrast, and a pill-shaped button. Approximate colours: background `#F8F7F4`, primary text `#171717`, accent pink `#F25A7B`, hand-drawn accent `#4D2448`, button `#171717`, button text `#FFFFFF`. Use a 24px outer gutter and a restrained max-width.

Never use the reference logo, product name, exact labels, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
