## Before you start
Ask which platforms and environments the user's product genuinely supports, and what proof of breadth matters to its buyers. Reinterpret this section for that ecosystem.

Create a full-width dark feature panel with a very large corner radius around 30–36px, using near-black `#171815` and cream text. Center a serif or editorial headline of roughly 27–34px, followed by one muted explanatory line. Below it, arrange supported integrations as compact rounded chips in several evenly spaced rows; each chip can include a tiny abstract colored mark and 11–12px label. Finish with two large light pill buttons for the primary operating-system or platform choices. Keep the panel padded generously and ensure chips wrap cleanly. Use subtle charcoal borders on chips and strong contrast on the platform buttons.

Never:
- use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference
- claim integrations that do not exist
- crowd the panel with more chips than users can scan
