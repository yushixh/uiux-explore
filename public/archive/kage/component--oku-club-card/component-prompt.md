## Before you start
Ask who is behind the user's product, what should be shared publicly, and whether the brand uses portraits, avatars, or abstract profile art. Build an original team story.

Design a centered team-introduction section. Use a serif heading, a readable paragraph limited to a moderate line length, then a three-column profile row with circular or softly masked portrait/illustration areas, names, and locations or roles. Keep the profile row simple and spacious; the story should establish credibility without becoming a résumé grid.

Use white `#ffffff`, near-black `#080808`, muted gray `#666666`, 28–38px heading, 14–16px paragraph, 48–72px gaps, and minimal borders. Ensure profile images have descriptive alt text and stack cleanly on mobile.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
