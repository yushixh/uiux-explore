## Before you start
Ask what the user's primary conversion goal is and what concise promise should motivate it. Then write original content for this pattern.

Create a spacious final CTA band on a black background with a faint centered grid or radial glow. Center a small uppercase eyebrow, a very large single-line or two-line headline, a concise muted paragraph, and two horizontally aligned action buttons. Keep the composition calm and high contrast, with no extra cards or imagery.

Use a 240–280px desktop height, 58–72px headline sizing, 14px body copy, 8px button radii, and a bright primary surface (#f2f2ef) against a dark secondary button (#171819). Provide responsive wrapping and stack the actions on narrow screens.

Never: use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, imagery, or exact CTA labels.
