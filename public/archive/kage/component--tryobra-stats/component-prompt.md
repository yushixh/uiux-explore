## Before you start
Ask what measurable outcome the user's product creates and which single statistic best builds trust. Use the product's own data and voice.

Build a centered proof-point band on a near-black background (#0b0d0d). Add a tiny uppercase eyebrow, one oversized orange metric, a bold off-white interpretation line, and a small muted qualification beneath. Use strong vertical rhythm: approximately 24px eyebrow spacing, 0.9 line-height for the metric, and generous empty space around it. Add thin orange rules or a small measurement motif without making the section decorative. Ensure the metric remains readable and responsive on mobile.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
