## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand voice and visual identity are. Apply the following principles to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Create a centered closing CTA with a short two-line heading in the product's accent color, a single light pill button, and generous top and bottom padding. Use a near-black background and a restrained lavender gradient such as `#b78cff` to `#e5b6ff`, but adapt it to the user's palette. Keep the heading around 36–44px, the button around 48px tall with 999px radius, and the composition intentionally sparse. Add a subtle top or bottom divider if it helps separate the CTA from the footer.

Ensure the action is specific, keyboard accessible, and still readable for users with color-vision differences.

### Never
- Never copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never add multiple competing conversion paths to a closing CTA.
