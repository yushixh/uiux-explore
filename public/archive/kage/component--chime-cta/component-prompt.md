## Before you start
Ask what conversion action matters most for the user's product and which platforms or channels should be promoted. Then design a branded CTA panel around that action.

Build a wide rounded CTA panel on a light page with a deep green gradient background around #003B2B to #07543A. Use a two-column composition: left has a small eyebrow, bold 32–40px heading, one-line supporting text, and two platform/download or action buttons; right has a single strong product visual with a slight tilt and a small floating proof badge. Use generous 64px desktop padding, 16px radius, bright green or white accents, and white text. Keep store buttons visually subordinate to the headline but easy to recognize, with consistent height and spacing. On mobile, stack the visual below the copy and keep the primary action above the fold.

Never: copy the reference's product name, headline, app-store badges, device render, proof claim, logos, imagery, or exact copy.
