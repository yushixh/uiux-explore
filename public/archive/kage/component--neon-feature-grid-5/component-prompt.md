## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to their identity rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a dark enterprise capability section with a small decorative accent, a large two-line statement, and a six-item grid below. Use three columns on desktop and stack on mobile. Each item has a small mint line icon, a short bold title, and a muted explanatory paragraph. Use vertical separators between columns and a subtle horizontal separator between rows, avoiding card shadows. Palette: #000, #f5f5f0, #7b817f, #38d6a2, #272d2b. Keep the grid compact, align all item starts, and provide hover/focus states that brighten the border or icon.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
