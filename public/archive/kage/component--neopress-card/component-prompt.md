## Before you start
Ask what principles, capabilities, and proof points define the user's product. Then turn those answers into an original long-form product philosophy section.

### Design language
- Place the content in a centered white card inside a subtly contrasting warm page background; constrain reading width and use a clear vertical narrative.
- Start with a tiny uppercase eyebrow and a concise serif or display heading, followed by readable sans-serif paragraphs. Use a three-column list block for capabilities with bold mini-headings and checkmarked bullets.
- Break the long content with a simple abstract process diagram or system map using thin lines, labels, and one small accent node; do not rely on illustration.
- Use `#ffffff` card surfaces, `#faf9f5` surrounding background, `#161616` text, `#6d6b65` secondary copy, `#dfdcd4` borders, and a muted coral accent (`#d96f55`).
- Keep generous internal padding, 1px separators, modest 2px corners, and strong mobile stacking. Use semantic headings and lists.

### Never
- Never reuse logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never fabricate founder claims, use a confusing diagram, or compress a long-form explanation into tiny type.
