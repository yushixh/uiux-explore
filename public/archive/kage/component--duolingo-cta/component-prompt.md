## Before you start
Ask what premium offering the user's product has, what upgrade promise is strongest, and what action should be taken, then apply the principles below to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a full-width premium CTA banner with a deep navy background (approximately #111044), a two-column layout, and a bold high-contrast headline on the right or center. Pair the message with a large abstract or product-relevant hero visual on the opposite side. Use bright lime green and electric violet accents for emphasis, white supporting text, and a single compact light button with dark text. Give the banner generous vertical padding, keep content within a max-width container, and ensure the color contrast meets accessibility standards. Add a subtle hover lift to the button and make the entire composition responsive by stacking on small screens.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
