## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a split promotional hero for a broad use-case library:
- Use a very light lavender/blue background with subtle radial gradients.
- Align a short headline, one supporting sentence, and a dark primary CTA in a left column.
- Fill the right column with a lively collage of rotated cards or product examples, using varied scales, slight overlap, and controlled clipping at the container edge.
- Keep the text column around 38–42% width and vertically center it; use 30–36px bold heading and 16px body text.
- Preserve depth with soft shadows and small rotation angles, but ensure the collage remains decorative and does not reduce accessibility.

Never:
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never reproduce the exact card collage.
