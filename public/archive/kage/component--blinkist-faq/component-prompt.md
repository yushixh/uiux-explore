## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build an FAQ section with a centered heading and a constrained content width of roughly 480–560px. Render questions as full-width accordion rows with bold dark-navy labels, thin light-gray separators #d9dfdd, generous 20–24px vertical padding, and a right-aligned chevron. Animate expansion gently, allow only one or several items open according to the product need, and use proper button semantics with aria-expanded and accessible panel IDs. Keep the background white and the section vertically generous.

Never use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
