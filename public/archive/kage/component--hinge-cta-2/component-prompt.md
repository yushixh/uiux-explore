## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a dark press/newsroom CTA with a small eyebrow, a large serif headline, one short explanatory sentence, and a high-contrast light pill button. Keep the content left aligned inside a wide container and use generous vertical padding. Finish with a thin horizontal divider and enough breathing room before the footer. Approximate colours: background `#191919`, heading `#F5F2EE`, body `#DDD8D2`, button `#FFFFFF`, button text `#171717`, divider `#454545`; use a 999px button radius and subtle hover inversion.

Never use the reference logo, product name, exact labels, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
