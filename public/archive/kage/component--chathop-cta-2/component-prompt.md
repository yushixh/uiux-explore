## Before you start
Ask what the ideal conversion is, whether it is free or paid, and what objections users have before starting. Use those answers to create the final CTA.

Build a bright closing CTA that follows the dark product story. Center a short, high-energy headline, place one prominent pill-shaped action directly beneath it, and add a compact reassurance line about setup, cost, or commitment. Include an original decorative product cue only if it supports the conversion and does not distract from the button.

Design language: use a pale lime background around #efffd5 with a warmer green glow near the top, near-black #050805 headline text, and a white or very pale button with black label for contrast. Headline should be 48–60px and bold; button 48–56px tall with 999px radius, subtle shadow, and clear hover/focus states. Use 80–110px vertical padding and keep the content max-width around 600px. Ensure the CTA remains readable in high contrast and stacks cleanly on mobile.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
