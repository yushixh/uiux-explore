## Before you start
Ask what final action the user's product wants visitors to take and what one-sentence promise can motivate that action. Build a focused closing CTA from that answer.

Create a wide rounded conversion banner with a vivid yellow top that softly fades toward cream. Use a large bold headline, a short supporting line, and one dark pill CTA aligned to the left. Add a tiny uppercase line of supported platforms or reassurance beneath the main content only if it is relevant. Use yellow `#ffd21a`, cream `#f5efe3`, black `#171714`, and muted brown `#6b624e`; use a 28px radius, 32px padding, 24–32px headline size, and ample breathing room. Keep this banner visually distinct but not noisy, and make the CTA the only dominant interactive element.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
