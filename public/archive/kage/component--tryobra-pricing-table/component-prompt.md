## Before you start
Ask what pricing model, billing cadence, entitlements, and primary conversion action fit the user's product. Do not assume the reference's plan names or prices.

Create a dark pricing section with a chapter label, large heading, and a monthly/annual toggle above a two-column plan comparison. Give the free plan a dark surface with a light border and the recommended or paid plan a vivid orange surface (#ff5425). Each plan needs a small label, large price, billing qualifier, concise feature checklist, and full-width CTA. Use strong contrast, 1px borders, square or 2px radii, 32px padding, and an accessible selected state for the billing toggle. On mobile, stack the plans while preserving comparison clarity.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
