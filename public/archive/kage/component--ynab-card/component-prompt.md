## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a standalone testimonial or founder-quote block with a very spacious pale background. Constrain the quote to a readable central or left-aligned column, use large italic text with highlighted or lightly tinted emphasis, and place the speaker's name and role beneath in a smaller upright style. Prioritize rhythm and whitespace over decoration. Use #fff9e9 or a similarly soft neutral background, #171957 text, 28–40px quote type with 1.15 line height, and a maximum text width around 600px. Add no card border; let the typography carry the section. Ensure the quote remains readable and scales down cleanly on mobile.

Never: reuse logos, product names, copy, illustrations, imagery, or exact branding from the reference.
