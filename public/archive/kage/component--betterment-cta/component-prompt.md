## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a centered closing CTA on a warm cream background. Use one concise dark-navy serif heading at approximately 36–48px, a single strong blue button beneath it, and ample empty space around the group. Keep the content max-width around 600px, use 72–100px vertical padding, and make the button at least 44px high with an 8px radius. Add a subtle hover lift or colour shift and a visible keyboard focus ring. Do not dilute this band with extra navigation or multiple competing offers.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
