## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to their identity rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a full-width dark hero beneath the navigation, approximately 820px tall. Layer an abstract, nonliteral technology texture behind the content with a strong black gradient for readability. Use a max-width 854px inner container, an understated eyebrow, an oversized 52–64px responsive headline with tight line-height, and two adjacent CTAs: filled light primary and outlined secondary. Below, add five evenly distributed capability blurbs with bold lead-ins and muted descriptions, followed by a horizontal strip of five small interface preview cards. Use #000/#050606, white #f5f5f2, gray #777c7b, neon green #39d5a1, and faint #1c2422 borders. Keep generous vertical rhythm and ensure the headline remains the focal point.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
