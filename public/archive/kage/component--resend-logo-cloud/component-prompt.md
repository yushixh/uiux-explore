## Before you start
Ask what the user's product is, who it serves, and what its brand voice and visual identity are. Apply the following principles to that product rather than reproducing the reference.

Build a centered social-proof band on a black background. Start with one concise sentence in small muted type, then place customer or integration marks in a balanced responsive grid across two rows. Render marks in monochrome white/gray with consistent visual height, generous column gaps, and enough breathing room to avoid a logo wall feeling crowded. Add a subtle text link below for a deeper enterprise story. Keep the section around 280–320px tall, use no cards or heavy borders, and allow horizontal wrapping or a marquee only if it remains accessible.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
