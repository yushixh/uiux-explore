## Before you start
Ask what app, platform availability, audience, and brand system the user's product has, then apply this layout to those needs.

Create a full-width app-download CTA with a bright blue background close to #4BAEF0. Center a very large black headline, place a scannable QR-code placeholder beneath it, and show a tall rounded smartphone/product preview rising from the lower portion of the section. Add an optional circular play or demo control to one side. Use a 64–80px headline on desktop, 32–44px on mobile, generous vertical padding, and a phone frame with approximately 28px radius and subtle shadow. Keep QR code sizing accessible, provide a text alternative and app-store links for non-scanners, and ensure the phone visual does not clip awkwardly at narrow widths.

### Never
- Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
- Never use the reference QR code, phone screenshot, or exact headline.
