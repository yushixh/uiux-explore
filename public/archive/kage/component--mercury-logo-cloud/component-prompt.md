## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the following principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Create a proof section on the same dark canvas. Center a two- or three-line statement about customer adoption, then arrange 5–7 monochrome customer marks beneath it with even spacing and subdued contrast. Follow with a testimonial carousel/card: use a rounded media panel, a short quote over the image or beside it, attribution, and a thin progress indicator. Keep logos as text placeholders or generic shapes for the implementation. Use white type, #a3a3b0 secondary text, #262632 cards, 10–14px radius, and generous vertical spacing. Make the testimonial slides keyboard accessible and show clear active-state progress.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
