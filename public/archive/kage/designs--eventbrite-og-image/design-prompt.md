## Before you start
Ask the user what product or brand this social card is for, who will be sharing the links and on which platforms, and what brand assets they already have (name, wordmark, brand colours, typeface). Also ask whether they want a logo-only card like the reference composition or a wordmark plus one short supporting line. Wait for the answers before designing; everything below is applied to *their* brand, not to the reference.

## Page structure
Produce a single 1200×630 image (HTML/CSS rendered to PNG, or a standalone SVG):
- **Full-bleed background**: one flat, solid colour filling the entire 1200×630 frame — no gradient, texture, border or vignette.
- **Single focal element**: the brand wordmark set as large lowercase text, occupying roughly 60–70% of the canvas width, horizontally centred and positioned just above the true vertical midpoint so it doesn't sag in cropped previews.
- **Optional supporting line**: only if the user asked for it — one short tagline or URL set very small (~28–36px) beneath the wordmark with generous spacing. The reference uses the wordmark alone.
- Nothing else: no chrome, no buttons, no corner marks, no footer strip.

## Design language
- **One hue on one neutral**: the entire card is a single accent colour against a single flat background (e.g. warm coral-orange ≈ `#EE5A3A` on white `#FFFFFF`, or inverted). No second accent, no imagery.
- **Type**: a geometric rounded sans-serif, all lowercase, with tight but even letter-spacing. Allow exactly one playful glyph detail — an angled or clipped ascender, a circular tittle — as the memorability hook; everything else stays disciplined.
- **Scale**: at 1200×630, the wordmark's letter height should land around 120–160px. Supporting text (if any) drops at least 4× smaller so the wordmark stays the only focal point.
- **Negative space as the layout system**: the content block should occupy under 40% of the canvas, with at least 150px of clear margin on every side. The emptiness is the design.
- **Flatness**: no shadows, gradients, strokes or borders anywhere; contrast and hierarchy come solely from colour against emptiness.
- **Optical centring**: nudge the wordmark a few pixels above mathematical centre; verify legibility at a 120×63 thumbnail, where the letter shapes must still read.

## Never
- Never reproduce the Eventbrite name, wordmark or its specific tilted-"t"/round-dot letterforms; substitute the user's own placeholder branding and copy.
- No illustrations, photography or third-party logo assets from the reference.
- Never present or label the finished card as Eventbrite's brand — it is a new card in that compositional style.
