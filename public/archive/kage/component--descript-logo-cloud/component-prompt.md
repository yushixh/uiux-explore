## Before you start
Ask which customers, partners, or communities the user's product can truthfully reference and what proof is most persuasive for its audience. Then build an original trust section.

Create a dark-background social-proof band with a one-line centered credibility statement, a horizontally distributed logo row, and three equal testimonial cards beneath it. Keep logos monochrome and subdued so they support rather than dominate the page. Make quote cards slightly darker than the surrounding background, with 18–24px padding, 8–12px radius, concise quotes, attribution, and a small organization label. Approximate palette: surrounding #43071b, cards #26050e, off-white text #fff5f0, muted text #d5aeb5, low-contrast logo color #f0dfe0. Use a max-width container, 24px column gaps, and stack cards on mobile. If logos are unavailable, use text-based customer categories or anonymized proof. Add a gentle carousel or horizontal scroll only on small screens if needed.

### Never
- Never use the reference's logos, company names, testimonials, or exact wording.
- Never fabricate endorsements; support anonymized or measurable proof instead.
