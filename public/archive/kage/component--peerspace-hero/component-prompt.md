## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then adapt this pattern to their product.

Create a full-bleed hero with a cinematic lifestyle image occupying the upper portion and a dark lower copy field. Place a prominent, horizontally segmented search form near the image-to-copy transition with fields for intent, location, timing, and a single filled submit button. Use an oversized bold sans-serif headline with tight line-height, white text on the dark panel, and a small editorial location/category caption aligned to the far edge. Preserve generous negative space and make the search bar collapse into stacked fields on mobile. Use approximately #111111, #FFFFFF, and a restrained neutral gray; use 12–16px radii on the form and buttons.

Never: use the reference logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or distinctive brand assets.
