## Before you start
Ask what legal links, attribution, social links, and trust signals the user's product needs. Then create a minimal original footer using the rules below.

Design a centered footer with a short attribution sentence, a compact inline row of legal and utility links, and an optional small trust or status badge beneath. Keep it visually separate with generous top padding but avoid a large colored band. Use a max-width around 500px and center-align all content; make links wrap cleanly on small screens.

Use #fffdf9, muted text #82796f, slightly darker links #5e5750, coral #df765e for hover or emphasis, and a subtle top border #eee7df. Use 11–12px body text, 10–11px footer links, 18–24px spacing between groups, 1px separators only where useful, and a compact 8–12px badge radius. Ensure every link has a visible hover/focus state and the badge has an accessible text alternative.

Never use the reference's attribution, product name, legal copy, badge design, logos, illustrations, or imagery.
