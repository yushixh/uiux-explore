## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply these principles to that product rather than copying the reference.

Build a dark navy statistics band (`#06145c`) with a horizontally arranged set of four or five pale cards. Each card should feature one very large number, a short plain-language label, and optional supporting icon; use 18–22px radius, minimal shadow, and generous padding. Allow the row to become a horizontally scrollable carousel or a two-column grid on mobile, with keyboard-accessible controls if it scrolls. Center a tiny, muted source/methodology note beneath the cards. Keep numbers visually dominant but ensure labels and units are explicit.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
