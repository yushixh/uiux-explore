## Before you start
Ask what objections and support questions users have before conversion. Organize original answers around the user's product and terminology.

Build a warm ivory FAQ section with a chapter eyebrow, large heading, and a vertical accordion list. Use full-width 1px dark borders, compact question IDs in orange monospace, bold question text, and a plus icon at the far right. Keep rows around 44–56px tall, support keyboard interaction, and animate expansion subtly without shifting the entire page unexpectedly. Add a small muted line and text link below for additional help. Use #f2f1e8 background, #111312 text, and #b44727/orange accents.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
