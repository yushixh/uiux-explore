## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to their identity rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a dark closing CTA band with a subtle abstract technical texture confined to the background. Use a large 48–60px headline on the left, a short supporting sentence beneath it, and three actions aligned toward the lower/right area: filled primary, outlined secondary, and a green command-style button with a copy affordance. Use #171817, #f5f5f0, #828886, #38d5a0, and #2a302e. Keep the CTA compact, maintain strong contrast, and make the command action copy to clipboard with a visible confirmation state.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
