## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to that product rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a generous two-column account callout with text on the left and a large contained visual on the right. Use a white page background, a 40–48px heading, short explanatory copy, a black primary button, and a low-emphasis secondary text link. Keep the visual in a pale neutral panel around `#f5f5f5` with a 12px radius and crop it cleanly rather than letting it dominate the text. Use a max-width around 1200px, balanced columns, 64–96px vertical padding, and a 32–48px gap. On mobile, stack the content and image, with the actions remaining adjacent where space allows. Add visible hover, focus, and loading states to the account action.

Never: use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
