## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply these principles to that context rather than reproducing the reference.

Create a calm, centered CTA section for a financial service. Place a tiny accent icon or eyebrow above a two-line heading, make the key numerical or benefit phrase prominent, add one short explanatory sentence, and finish with a very small qualification note. Use a white background, centered max-width of about 520px, near-black serif heading (#111111), muted sans-serif copy (#666666), and plenty of vertical whitespace. The section should feel like a persuasive pause rather than a loud sales banner; if a button is needed, use one small dark pill below the explanatory copy.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
