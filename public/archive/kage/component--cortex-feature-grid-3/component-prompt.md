## Before you start
Ask which capabilities distinguish the user's product and what visual metaphors fit its brand. Create original cards using this system.

Build an asymmetric feature-card grid after a large left-aligned section heading. Put a small uppercase eyebrow above a bold two-line headline, with a short explanatory paragraph aligned to the right. Below, create a bordered grid of five cards: one large spanning card, two compact cards on the top row, and two cards on the lower row with one wider card. Each card should include a tiny index/category label, a restrained visual demonstration, a bold benefit heading, and muted supporting copy.

Use near-black surfaces (#0b0c0d), borders #252729, 8–10px radii, and 24–32px internal padding. Maintain consistent card heights and align headings along a grid. Visuals may be abstract UI blocks, nodes, code fields, or language tiles, but they should be simple and product-relevant. Add subtle hover border brightening and mobile stacking.

Never: use the reference's logos, product names, copy, illustrations, imagery, or exact card content.
