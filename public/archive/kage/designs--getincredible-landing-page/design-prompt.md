# Incredible-style waitlist landing page

## Before you start
Ask the user: what product are they launching, who is it for, and what brand assets (name, logo, colours, typeface) do they already have? Wait for answers before building. Everything below is applied to their product, not to the reference.

## Page structure
1. **Top nav**: slim white bar — small black wordmark/logo left, single black pill button ("Join the waitlist" style CTA) right. No menu links; the page is pre-launch.
2. **Hero card**: one large rounded-corner (~40px radius) banner card inset with margins, filled with a diagonal gradient from vivid lime/chartreuse (~#c8f542) bottom-left to pale cream (~#faf5ea) top-right. Inside, left-aligned stack: small white pill tag ("A new chapter for …"), massive bold black headline across three short lines (~72–90px, tight leading, near-zero tracking), one plain subline (~18px), and a black pill "Read more" button.
3. **Empty breathing space**: large white gap after the card — intentional restraint, don't fill it.
4. **Secondary CTA block**: left-aligned black pill button ("Download …") with an underlined text link beneath it.
5. **Legal / fine print**: 2–3 paragraphs of very small (~11–12px) grey regulatory text — company registration numbers, FCA references, disclaimers — full-width, left aligned.
6. **Footer links**: small underlined links (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy).
7. **Copyright line**: tiny grey "© year Company Ltd" at the very bottom.

## Design language
- Hierarchy is carried almost entirely by type scale: one gigantic headline vs tiny legal print, with almost nothing in between.
- Use a geometric/rounded bold sans (e.g. something like Poppins/Manrope bold, weight 700–800) for the headline; regular weight of the same family for body; same family at 11–12px for legal text.
- Colour discipline: black (#111) and white only, plus a single accent lime (#c8f542) used inside the hero gradient; no other colours.
- Gradient rule: one accent gradient per page, inside a large rounded card, never behind the whole page.
- Buttons are solid black pills (fully rounded, ~14px vertical padding) with white text; secondary affordances are plain underlined links.
- Generous whitespace: the page is short; let the gap between hero and footer be larger than feels necessary.
- All content is left-aligned to a single container (~1200px max) — no centred sections, no columns, no imagery.
- Motion: none visible; keep hover states to simple opacity or subtle scale on pills.

## Never
- Never reuse the "Incredible" name, wordmark, or the "Financial admin is never fun. So leave it to us." copy.
- Never copy the reference's legal text or regulatory claims.
- Don't add stock photography, illustrations, icon sets, or feature grids — the design's power is its sparseness.
