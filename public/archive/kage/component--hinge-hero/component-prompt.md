## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to their context rather than reproducing this reference.

Create a full-width hero immediately below the navigation, approximately 450px tall on desktop. Use a high-quality product-relevant image or video as a cover background with a darkened gradient where text sits. Place one large editorial serif headline in the lower-left, with tight line-height, a maximum width of about 600px, and white text. Preserve a clear focal point in the image and keep the headline readable without covering the main subject. Use responsive typography that scales down on mobile; keep the hero's content vertically anchored near the bottom. Approximate colours: text `#FFFFFF`, subtle overlay `rgba(0,0,0,.18)`.

Never use the reference logo, product name, exact labels, copy, illustrations, or imagery.
