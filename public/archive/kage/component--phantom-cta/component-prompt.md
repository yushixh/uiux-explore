## Before you start
Ask what the user's desired conversion is, what proof or reassurance should precede it, and what action label matches the product. Then implement an original version.

Build a centered CTA section with a short credibility sentence, a very large two-line headline, and one rounded primary button beneath it. Use generous vertical spacing and a pale lavender background, approximately #F5F3FF, with dark indigo typography around #302B50. The headline should be 56–72px on desktop with tight line height and responsive scaling; keep supporting text smaller and muted. The button should be white or lightly tinted, pill-shaped, softly shadowed, and include a small contextual icon only if useful. Align everything centrally and ensure the section has enough bottom padding to feel like a deliberate transition into the footer.

Never: copy logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
