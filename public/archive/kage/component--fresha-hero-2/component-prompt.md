## Before you start
Ask what the user's product and brand are, then apply the principles below to that context rather than reproducing this reference.

Build a compact centered trust statement section. Use a bold 28–36px headline, one supporting sentence below it, and generous vertical padding so it reads as a deliberate transition rather than a banner. Keep the background white, text near-black (`#111111`), and alignment centered within a max-width of 720px. Maintain a clear hierarchy and avoid adding imagery when the message itself is the focus.

Never: use the reference's logo, product name, exact copy, illustrations, imagery, or branding.
