## Before you start
Ask what the user's product, audience, and brand are, then apply the principles below to their product rather than copying this reference.

Create a centered hero for a developer-focused SaaS product. Place a large responsive headline in two lines, a narrow supporting paragraph below it, then a primary light button and secondary outlined button. Add a small compatibility or integration strip beneath the actions, followed by a wide framed product preview that visually demonstrates the promise. Use a black-to-charcoal background with a very subtle green atmospheric glow, white headline text around #f1f3f1, muted copy around #929896, mint accent around #aef0c7, and surfaces around #0c1010. Keep the hero vertically spacious, with the visual beginning below the CTA row; use 16–20px outer radius, faint 1px borders, soft shadows, and restrained glow. The headline should be 48–72px desktop with tight line-height and scale smoothly on mobile. Buttons should have strong contrast, 12px radius, and arrow affordances; the preview may show abstract panes, status indicators, and agent activity without relying on literal screenshots.

Never use logos, product names, copy, illustrations, or imagery from the reference.
